# Islamic study specialization

Use for Qur'an, hadith, tafsir, 'aqidah, fiqh, seerah, usul, Arabic-Islamic terminology, scholar/teacher lessons, and related Islamic study.

This is a **study-process fidelity module**. It does not issue independent religious rulings.

## Source-layer inheritance

Preserve, where material:
- `Q` — Qur'an;
- `H` — hadith / Prophetic report;
- `Ath` — athar / salaf statement;
- `U` — named scholar/view;
- `T` — teacher explanation;
- `Me` — learner explanation/reflection;
- `?` — uncertain source/wording/reference.

Use the note-taking Islamic specialization for capture/revision fidelity.

## Study-priority vs religious obligation

The skill may say:
- "high study priority";
- "worth active recall";
- "foundational in this lesson";
- "teacher emphasized this";
- "useful to memorize exactly if verified."

The skill must not say:
- "religiously obligatory to memorize";
- "this must be memorized in Islam";
unless that claim is explicitly supported by the lesson or separately verified through an appropriate Islamic authority/source process.

## Exact memorization gate

Before `EXACT` practice for Qur'an, hadith or scholar wording:
1. establish exact source text from the supplied/verified source;
2. preserve source identity;
3. distinguish quote from translation/paraphrase;
4. do not repair uncertain wording from model memory;
5. mark unresolved material for verification.

A paraphrase may be studied for meaning but not silently promoted to an exact quote.

## What not to flatten

If material, retain:
- conditions;
- exceptions;
- scope;
- negation;
- disagreement;
- identity of view-holder;
- attributed tarjih;
- link between dalil/evidence and claim.

A short recall prompt is invalid if success on it would teach a materially overbroad ruling.

## Target examples

### UNDERSTAND
Explain the teacher's explanation in your own words, then compare with the lesson.

### RECALL
Recall the lesson's named foundations, categories, conditions or sequence.

### EXACT
Only verified wording whose exact form is actually part of the study goal.

### EVIDENCE_CHAIN
`mas'alah -> claim/ruling -> dalil -> link -> condition/exception -> khilaf -> attributed tarjih`

Use only the parts actually present/material in the lesson.

### DISCRIMINATE
Distinguish:
- transmitted wording vs teacher explanation;
- one scholarly view vs another;
- condition-present vs condition-absent cases.

## Learner-generation boundary

The learner may generate:
- own-word explanation;
- recall of a structure;
- comparison;
- application to a teacher-provided example.

Keep learner wording labeled as understanding.
Do not let it become Qur'an, hadith, tafsir, scholarly quotation or consensus by drift.

## Review correction

If recall changes source/authority level:
- classify as `SOURCE_ERROR` even when the general idea seems close;
- return to source;
- repair before further rehearsal.

For Islamic study, fidelity errors can matter more than brevity or fluency.
