# Anti-doping & product-quality reference

Version: 0.1
Reviewed: 2026-09-22.

## Current-authority rule

Anti-doping status is versioned. The 2026 WADA Prohibited List is effective from 1 January 2026, but runtime questions must still verify the current list/date rather than assuming this file remains current.

## Medication status

For athletes subject to testing:
- use the current WADA Prohibited List;
- where supported, use Global DRO for specific medications and country/product context;
- consider route, dose/threshold, in-competition vs out-of-competition status, and TUE rules when applicable.

Do not assume that a medication not found in one database is permitted.

Global DRO does not cover dietary supplements.

## Dietary supplements

A supplement can create anti-doping risk because:
- the label can be incomplete or inaccurate;
- formulations can change;
- contamination/adulteration can occur;
- the same brand may differ by market or batch.

Never say:
- "WADA approved supplement";
- "safe for doping tests" solely from the ingredient list;
- "not on a high-risk list, therefore safe".

Third-party certification can reduce risk but does not create a zero-risk guarantee.

USADA currently recommends NSF Certified for Sport as its preferred risk-reduction certification program for athletes, but this is a USADA recommendation rather than a universal regulatory guarantee.

## Product review workflow

When the user gives a branded product:
1. identify exact product name, market/country and current label;
2. record serving size and servings actually used;
3. enumerate active ingredients and doses;
4. flag proprietary blends / undisclosed amounts;
5. look for drug-like, stimulant, prohormone, SARM or novel compounds;
6. check current regulator/anti-doping sources when relevant;
7. distinguish product efficacy from purity/eligibility.

If the formulation cannot be verified, say so.

## Legal/regulatory status

Do not generalize US FDA supplement rules to other jurisdictions.

When legality, prescription status, importation or sale is material:
- retrieve current official authority for the relevant jurisdiction;
- state the jurisdiction and effective/current date;
- if reliable official evidence is unavailable, do not guess.

## Evasion boundary

Do not help users:
- time use to avoid detection;
- choose masking agents;
- manipulate urine/blood testing;
- conceal prohibited use.

You may explain the rule and consequences at a high level.
