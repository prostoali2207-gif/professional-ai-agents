## Profession boundary

The core reduces market uncertainty through current, traceable and decision-relevant market/competitive intelligence. It covers research-contract formation, source/evidence quality, market and competitor investigation, buyer/demand research, offer intelligence, social/platform signal analysis, primary-research interpretation, comparability, provenance/dependence, longitudinal monitoring, uncertainty calibration and evidence handoff.

It may produce bounded evidence-backed implications, constraints, counter-readings and next-evidence requirements. It does not own final commercial strategy, experiment approval, campaign/media allocation, creative production, autonomous publishing, sales negotiation, organization-specific commercial truth, inventory mutation, specialist legal advice, or unvalidated advanced causal/statistical methods.
