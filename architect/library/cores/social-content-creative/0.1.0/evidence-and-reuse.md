# Social Content Creative Practitioner — Evidence and Reuse Record 0.1.0

Status: candidate support artifact. Not qualification evidence.

## Profession reconstruction
The target is not a universal “SMM manager.” The reconstructed stable work is a bounded creative profession that converts an already-approved communication brief and verified evidence into exact copy plus shoot-ready social/video creative execution.

Adjacent professions remain separate:
- Strategist: audience, objective, offer, KPI, experiment portfolio and decision rule.
- Market Intelligence: current market/competitor/customer evidence.
- Content Analyst: upstream content structure/mechanics where the applied system delegates that layer separately.
- Paid Media: spend, targeting, placements and paid-media optimization.
- Community/Listening/Reputation: comments, moderation, listening and reputation cases.
- Sales/Lead Conversion: buyer dialogue and closing path.
- Video Post-Production: edit/render/finish/QC.
- Analytics: performance/causal interpretation and SCALE/ITERATE/KILL.
- Publisher/authorized human: release approval and publishing.

## Evidence model
Primary research record: `architect/research/social-content-creative/evidence-register-v0.2.md`.

Evidence classes include:
- UAE primary law/regulation for deceptive advertising and used-goods disclosure risks;
- peer-reviewed advertising/consumer research for originality+relevance, divergence/convergence and message-execution effects;
- professional/academic video-storytelling sources for shot/story planning;
- official Meta, YouTube and Telegram documentation for live platform bindings;
- W3C for caption/accessibility principles;
- WIPO for idea-expression/reference/IP boundaries.

No single source is treated as a universal recipe. Creative judgment is triangulated and remains evaluation-dependent.

## Stable versus live separation
Stable core:
- brief fidelity;
- truthful persuasion and claim calibration;
- hook/payoff alignment;
- contextual copy judgment;
- divergence/convergence/critique;
- visual storytelling intent and production feasibility;
- reference literacy;
- experiment integrity;
- handoff and authority boundaries.

Live/contextual:
- platform formats, features, safe zones, durations, music/caption rules and recommendation behavior;
- UAE/current jurisdiction rules;
- organization facts, inventory, price, mileage, warranty, finance, permissions and brand voice;
- experiment IDs/locks and production resources.

## Evaluation obligations
Frozen public protocol: `architect/research/social-content-creative/qualification-protocol-v0.1.md`.

Qualification must include deterministic critical gates plus blinded comparative judgment evaluation. Critical failures cannot be averaged away by aesthetic quality.

High-risk held-out coverage includes missing/stale/conflicting commercial facts, misleading implication, fake scarcity/testimonial, brochure-vs-unit evidence, one-variable A/B locks, platform adaptation drift, incomplete briefs, noisy immature metrics, impossible assets and unauthorized self-approval.

## Portability contract
A target specialization may reuse this core only if it preserves the profession boundary and supplies compatible:
- brief/strategy ownership;
- authoritative fact packet;
- live platform/jurisdiction retrieval;
- experiment-lock contract when relevant;
- production resource state;
- downstream approval/publish boundary.

New domain claims, changed authority/tool bindings or material runtime differences require compatibility analysis and affected/new evaluation. A future PASS on this candidate would be prior evidence, not a transferable certificate.

## Limitations
- Candidate 0.1.0 is not qualified yet.
- Creative-quality competence cannot be proven by prose/static inspection alone.
- Organic Instagram performance claims are not inferred from paid Reels effect sizes.
- Legal rights/compliance interpretation remains outside the core.
- The candidate does not prove actual filming/editing/publishing competence; those require their respective human/runtime/downstream owners.
