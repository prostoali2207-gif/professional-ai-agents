Status: candidate support artifact. Not qualification evidence.

## Reconstruction evidence

### Stable profession evidence

**SPIN / consultative discovery**

Operational claim retained: strong discovery uses purposeful questioning to surface needs and decision consequences; Situation, Problem, Implication and Need-payoff are useful question-purpose categories but should not become a rigid interrogation script.

Evidence reviewed:

- Huthwaite-derived SPIN material and secondary professional summaries describing question sequencing and the risk of excessive situation questions.
- This evidence supports question-purpose logic, not a universal automotive script, cadence or qualification checklist.

**Customer-effort / next-step logic**

Operational claim retained: a sales interaction should advance the buyer by reducing uncertainty or moving to a proportionate next commitment; a proxy such as appointment count should not override buyer fit or unresolved blockers.

This is encoded as professional judgment and must be validated behaviorally rather than treated as a numerical benchmark.

### Automotive context evidence

Cox Automotive’s 2025 Car Buyer Journey study, released January 13, 2026, surveyed 2,300 U.S. consumers who bought a new or used vehicle in the prior 12 months. Public reporting emphasizes transparency, digital/omnichannel efficiency and personalization as contributors to buyer satisfaction.

Use boundary: contextual support that automotive sales spans digital and physical stages and that transparency matters. Do not import U.S. numeric benchmarks or infer UAE buyer behavior from this sample.

NADA’s June 23, 2025 discussion of Cox Automotive Car Buyer Journey findings similarly treats the dealership experience as a journey before, during and after the transaction. Use only as contextual support for multi-stage journey continuity.

### UAE specialization evidence

UAE Federal Law No. 15 of 2020 on Consumer Protection:

- defines misleading advertising as advertising based on misleading information or omission of fundamental/core information affecting the consumer’s contracting decision;
- requires advertised prices not to be misleading;
- prohibits describing a commodity/service with false data or advertising it misleadingly.

Source authorities reviewed:

- UAE Legislation portal: https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1455
- UAE Ministry of Economy consumer-protection law page: https://www.moec.gov.ae/en/federal-law-no-15-of-2020-on-consumer-protection

Use boundary: jurisdiction-specific rule for UAE deployment. The reusable core retains only the stable professional principle “do not fabricate or misrepresent material commercial facts”; current legal interpretation remains LIVE / specialist-owned.

## Evidence validity notes

- Sales-method research predates modern messaging channels; only profession-level judgment that remains mechanism-compatible is retained.
- Automotive buyer-journey evidence cited above is U.S.-population evidence and cannot establish UAE rates, channel preferences or conversion benchmarks.
- UAE law is authoritative for local legal text, but the core does not perform legal interpretation.
- No source is used to justify exact follow-up cadence, lead-temperature thresholds or universal qualification fields. Those remain deployment hypotheses/policy.
