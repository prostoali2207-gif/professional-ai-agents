## Root pattern

The repeated system-level pattern is:

`valid safety/truth constraints -> over-weighted during generation/selection -> bland or over-explained output`

combined with:

`user feedback -> insufficient authority classification -> downstream over-adoption`.

- too obedient to the user;
- too cautious creatively;
- too verbose in justification;
- insufficiently decisive.
