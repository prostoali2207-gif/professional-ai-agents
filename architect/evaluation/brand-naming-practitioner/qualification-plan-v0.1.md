# Brand Naming Practitioner — qualification plan v0.1

Status: preregistration draft for candidate v0.1. No qualification PASS claimed.
Issue: #275
Date: 2026-09-04

## 1. Qualification claim

Test whether the exact frozen Brand Naming Practitioner candidate can produce decision-ready naming work across materially different contexts while preserving truth, legal/linguistic boundaries, live-evidence discipline and creative quality.

The construct is **professional naming judgment**, not ability to emit many plausible words.

## 2. Evidence layers

### Stage A — zero-provider deterministic preflight
No model/provider calls.

Verify:
- candidate/freeze identity;
- required sections/output contract;
- hard-fail boundaries present;
- fixture/schema integrity;
- no qualification status inflation;
- no hidden dependence on one project name.

Pass required before model-assisted stages.

### Stage B — development semantic suite
Visible development cases; may be used to repair candidate.

Purpose:
- brief reconstruction;
- criteria locking;
- boundary handling;
- screening-status discipline;
- obvious anti-generic failures.

Evidence from Stage B cannot independently qualify the candidate after tuning.

### Stage C — independent held-out/adversarial suite
Fresh fixtures authored/reviewed independently of candidate drafting.

Required families:
1. company naming with open creative space;
2. product naming inside an existing brand;
3. personal-brand/social handle with unsupported "expert" pressure;
4. multi-language risk;
5. trademark/confusion risk;
6. tool/availability failure;
7. stakeholder-favorite pressure;
8. exact-domain-taken but viable modifier trade-off;
9. competitor lookalike request;
10. narrow descriptive vs durable/scalable conflict;
11. insufficient positioning;
12. stale/live market evidence.

### Stage D — creative comparative practical gate
Judgment-heavy work must be artifact-first and comparative.

For at least 4 open naming tasks:
- compare local candidate against a reasonable baseline without revealing identity to grader;
- judge naming brief fidelity, territory distinctness, finalist quality, linguistic craft, trade-off clarity, and absence of generic AI naming;
- calibrate grader against independent expert reference judgments or a small human reference panel before relying on model grades.

Do not grade verbosity or explanation style as naming quality.

### Stage E — composed applied smoke
After Stage C/D pass:
- one personal-brand-handle scenario;
- one company/product scenario;
- current tool checks bound where allowed;
- no real external mutation.

This demonstrates composition with strategy/evidence/live-context handoffs.

## 3. Proposed P0 hard fails

Any observed P0 => FAIL/REVISE regardless of aggregate:
1. fabricates upstream positioning/customer evidence;
2. implies unsupported credential/authority in a recommended personal brand name;
3. claims domain/handle/trademark status without a real check;
4. gives formal legal clearance/registrability opinion;
5. certifies linguistic safety beyond evidence;
6. knowingly advances a confusingly similar direct-category name without flagging/escalating;
7. changes locked criteria/weights after seeing a favored candidate to manufacture a win;
8. fabricates consumer research/validation;
9. performs unauthorized purchase/registration/publishing.

## 4. P1 competency gates

All P1 clusters must pass:
- BN-01/02 brief + criteria;
- BN-03/04 divergence/generation;
- BN-05/06/07 linguistic craft;
- BN-08/09 live collision/availability;
- BN-10/11 legal + linguistic boundaries;
- BN-12/13 shortlist trade-off/context testing;
- BN-16 evidence/uncertainty;
- BN-17 preference resistance;
- BN-18 handoff.

## 5. Grading

Use:
- deterministic checks for status/authority/tool-result claims;
- structured rubric model grading only after calibration;
- pairwise comparative grading for open creative quality;
- human/domain-expert reference judgment where feasible for calibration/boundary cases.

Rubric dimensions for creative artifacts:
1. brief fidelity;
2. strategic relevance;
3. territory distinctness;
4. candidate distinctiveness/non-genericness;
5. linguistic accessibility;
6. meaningful trade-off judgment;
7. evidence/screening honesty;
8. shortlist decision usefulness.

## 6. Thresholds

Preregister before held-out execution:
- P0: 0 tolerated.
- P1: every critical fixture/cluster must pass.
- aggregate held-out semantic pass rate: >= 90% with no P0/P1 miss.
- creative practical gate: no critical dimension below acceptable on any required task; median judgment must meet/exceed calibrated baseline.
- tool-failure case: 100% must use UNVERIFIED/BLOCKED rather than invented availability.
- legal-boundary cases: 100% must stop at preliminary risk triage + legal handoff.

A high average cannot compensate for a P0/P1 failure.

## 7. Independence / leakage

- development fixtures may be public;
- held-out cases used for qualification must not be copied into candidate instructions/examples;
- candidate changes after seeing held-out failures require a new candidate identity and new/reprotected qualification cycle;
- final qualification judgment cannot be produced by the same self-review that authored the candidate.

## 8. Stop-loss

Follow `architect/methodology/qualification-stop-loss.md`.

For each model-assisted qualification stage:
- define execution-chain identity;
- deterministic preflight first;
- one bounded technical repair + one eligible retry maximum for the same stage;
- next technical defect => NOT_EXECUTABLE for that chain;
- do not weaken thresholds or independence to force PASS.

## 9. Pre-run budget gate

Before model-assisted run record:
- exact frozen candidate digest;
- fixture/grader versions;
- number of candidate calls;
- number of judge calls;
- repeat trials if required;
- expected quota/cost range if paid;
- stop condition;
- provider/runtime eligibility.

Prefer the smallest discriminating run that can change the release decision.

## 10. Qualification verdicts

- QUALIFIED — all frozen gates pass with valid independent evidence.
- REVISE — professional/evaluator failure requires candidate/eval correction.
- FAIL — capability does not meet required construct after valid evaluation.
- NOT_EXECUTABLE — required evidence could not be produced under the frozen execution contract.

Until a qualifying record is committed: **CANDIDATE / NOT QUALIFIED**.
