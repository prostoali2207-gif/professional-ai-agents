# Brand Naming Practitioner v0.2 — evaluation plan

Status: preregistration draft for a new candidate revision. No qualification PASS claimed.
Issue: #282
Date: 2026-09-05

## 1. Revision claim

Test whether a materially revised Brand Naming Practitioner can preserve the v0.1 truth/legal/evidence boundaries while repairing applied failures in:

- anti-anchoring and creative divergence;
- naturalness/authenticity vs performative cleverness;
- personal identity-type first read;
- collision relevance;
- spoken-to-written robustness;
- polysemy/segmentation;
- personal-handle discovery-path weighting;
- comparative consistency.

v0.1 terminal NOT_EXECUTABLE evidence is not reinterpreted as professional failure or PASS.

## 2. Evaluation architecture

### Stage A — deterministic/static v0.2 preflight
Verify:
- new candidate identity/version;
- v0.1 files untouched;
- explicit v0.2 delta/evidence links;
- required new output fields;
- P0 boundaries retained;
- development fixture integrity;
- no copied applied candidate names used as hidden gold;
- no qualification-status inflation.

### Stage B — visible targeted development/regression
Use transformed cases that exercise failure mechanisms without treating exact chat wording as professional gold.

Required families:
1. anti-anchoring after stakeholder suggests a vivid territory;
2. standalone divergence without Social Content Creative;
3. naturalness vs performative cleverness;
4. personal alias vs place/project first read;
5. role/title vs person identity;
6. unrelated semantic noise vs material same-category collision;
7. homophone/transcription ambiguity;
8. lowercase segmentation ambiguity;
9. polysemy / unintended first meaning;
10. discovery-path weighting for personal social handles;
11. metamorphic collision-consistency pair;
12. retained v0.1 truth/legal/availability boundary;
13. decision economy / ceremonial-overanalysis trap;
14. bland-safe convergence vs magnetic-without-contrivance.

Stage B is repair evidence only and cannot independently qualify v0.2.

### Stage C — fresh independent held-out/adversarial suite
Must be authored/reviewed independently after the candidate is frozen.

Do not reuse exact applied names or close paraphrases as hidden answer tokens.

Required construct coverage:
- company;
- product;
- personal brand name-led;
- personal brand person-alias;
- concept-led personal identity;
- multilingual/cultural risk;
- legal/confusion boundary;
- tool failure;
- open creative brief;
- anchoring pressure;
- collision-relevance contrast;
- transcription ambiguity;
- performative-cleverness temptation;
- identity-type mismatch;
- insufficient strategy.

### Stage D — creative comparative practical gate

Creative quality must not be inferred from rule presence.

For at least 6 open naming tasks:
- blind-compare v0.2 against frozen v0.1 and a reasonable general baseline where feasible;
- randomize pair order;
- evaluate the produced naming artifacts, not explanation verbosity;
- use at least two independent judgments for highly subjective cases.

Dimensions:
1. brief fidelity;
2. material territory diversity;
3. finalist distinctiveness;
4. naturalness / authenticity to brief;
5. originality without contrivance;
6. identity-mode fit / first-read correctness;
7. linguistic accessibility;
8. oral-to-typed robustness;
9. collision-relevance judgment;
10. trade-off clarity and recommendation usefulness.

Critical creative defects:
- all territories are user-anchored variants;
- winner reads as the wrong identity type when the brief makes that material;
- obvious homophone/transcription defect is unreported;
- unrelated semantic noise is treated as material collision while comparable cases are not;
- “cleverness” is defended mainly by rationale after the name itself fails naturalness/fit.

### Stage E — human/practitioner calibration requirement

The v0.1 cycle explicitly lacked a human naming panel.

Before a v0.2 QUALIFIED claim on subjective creative quality:
- calibrate the creative rubric on a small blind reference set reviewed by at least one credible naming/brand practitioner or an equivalent independently justified human reference process;
- preserve disagreements rather than forcing consensus;
- if no human/practitioner calibration can be obtained, v0.2 may accumulate development evidence but must not claim full creative-quality qualification from model judges alone.

### Stage F — applied smoke

After independent semantic + creative gates pass:
- one personal-handle scenario with live screening;
- one company/product scenario;
- no external purchase/register/publish;
- verify handoff state and evidence labels.

## 3. Retained P0 hard fails

Zero tolerance:
1. fabricated positioning/customer research;
2. unsupported credential/authority in a recommended personal identity;
3. guessed domain/handle/trademark availability as fact;
4. formal legal clearance/registrability claim;
5. unsupported linguistic/cultural certification;
6. unflagged material direct/related-category confusion;
7. criteria manipulation to rescue a favorite;
8. fabricated consumer validation;
9. unauthorized external mutation.

## 4. New P1 gates

### P1-A — divergence independence
When the brief is open and the stakeholder proposes one territory, the candidate must still preserve materially different exploration before convergence.

### P1-B — naturalness / contrivance
Candidate must distinguish:
- distinctive and natural;
- distinctive but conspicuously engineered;
- familiar but bland;
without treating any one axis as universally dominant.

### P1-C — identity-type fit
Candidate must state likely first-read identity type for serious personal-brand finalists when material and surface mismatches.

### P1-D — collision relevance
Candidate must distinguish ordinary semantic noise / unrelated titles from real identity confusion and legal escalation.

### P1-E — oral/typed robustness
Homophone, alternate spelling, segmentation and likely first-meaning risks must be inspected before a candidate can rank first.

### P1-F — context weighting
Findability/availability criteria must reflect actual discovery paths and task type.

### P1-G — consistency
Metamorphic pairs that differ only in irrelevant category context should receive consistent professional treatment.

### P1-H — decision economy
The candidate must suppress low-value caveats/alternatives, avoid equating complexity with professionalism, and stop exploration when extra search cannot plausibly change the decision.

### P1-I — magnetism without contrivance
The candidate must distinguish clean-but-bland from genuinely magnetic and from visibly contrived naming. Anti-contrivance must not systematically collapse open personal-brand work into generic-safe constructions.

## 5. Proposed thresholds

Development/regression:
- 14/14 required families PASS;
- zero retained P0 violations.

Independent semantic:
- every critical family PASS;
- zero P0;
- no P1 family miss.

Creative practical:
- v0.2 must be preferred to frozen v0.1 on at least 4/6 blind open-creative tasks;
- no critical creative defect;
- no dimension systematically below acceptable across repeated tasks;
- preference evidence must include rationale tied to the named rubric dimensions.

Reliability:
- repeat at least the highest-variance creative/anchoring cases in two independent trials.

These are preregistration targets and may be revised only before any scored v0.2 candidate execution, with evaluator-owned rationale.

## 6. Independence / leakage

- exact chat candidates may be used in the production-failure dossier and visible development analysis;
- fresh held-out must use different names/mechanisms;
- candidate instructions must encode generalized professional rules, not answers to specific fixtures;
- any candidate mutation after scored held-out exposure requires a new candidate identity and new protected held-out.

## 7. Stop-loss

v0.2 is a new professional candidate revision, not a retry of v0.1.

Do not run a new provider-backed qualification merely because this candidate exists.

Before any scored remote execution:
- check current qualification-platform STOP policy;
- require a valid execution route;
- define one execution chain;
- static/preflight first;
- one bounded technical repair + one eligible retry maximum for that stage;
- preserve professional thresholds and independence.

Until fresh independent evidence satisfies the gate: **CANDIDATE / NOT QUALIFIED**.
