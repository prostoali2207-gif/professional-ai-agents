## Root-cause repairs discovered during validation

Validation did not simply confirm the initial candidate. It exposed defects in both the evaluation harness and Agent Architect behavior.

Harness defects were separated from candidate defects and invalidated runs were not retroactively relabeled PASS. These included MCP registry-policy incompatibility, incorrect MCP preflight assumptions, fixture-tool discoverability, telemetry serialization, and overly lexical graders. Fresh held-out variants were used after material grader/interface repairs.

A genuine B1 behavioral failure was also found: the Architect rejected an irrelevant distractor semantically but copied its raw value into durable-memory notes. The root cause was insufficient payload minimization at the memory write boundary. `SKILL.md` and `methodology/runtime-state-memory-context.md` were repaired so rejected raw payloads cannot survive indirectly in durable notes, provenance, audit comments, summaries, or exclusion metadata. Fresh B1 and B6 adversarial variants then passed in the final one-SHA release round.

## What PASS means

This PASS means the v1.1 benchmark claims added by the 2026-08 external benchmark now have representative behavioral evidence under the defined controlled runtime and held-out gate.

It does **not** mean the Architect is perfect, exhaustive, universally portable, or automatically transfers competence to applied agents. Each applied agent still requires profession-specific evidence, tools, live research where needed, practical evaluation, adversarial testing, and its own release decision.
