## Root-cause classification

v0.2 already knew the correct rules: mobile must be authored, proof must not be invented, references must not be imitated, and visual authority must not absorb UX/product/CRO decisions. v0.2 also introduced explicit veto gates.

R4 shows that knowledge plus late ready-state self-checks is still insufficient. A material decision can enter a direction, recommendation, implementation contract, or critique repair before the final gate is evaluated. Once admitted, the model may rationalize or preserve the move while still producing polished professional prose.

Repair layer: **professional judgment + execution control + evidence admission**.

The repair changes when a material decision becomes eligible for use. It does not suppress divergence, unconventional art direction, advanced media, justified rule-breaking, or creative risk inside the feasible solution space.

## J-06 — Material-decision admission control

Principle: a material visual decision is not eligible merely because it is aesthetically strong. Before it can enter a shortlisted direction, selected thesis, implementation contract, or bounded repair, it must survive the constraints that decision can affect.

Apply this admission control to material moves that affect one or more of:
- required function or communication;
- mobile/narrow-screen behavior;
- factual/business/product proof;
- reference-derived expression;
- UX/product/CRO/funnel behavior;
- accessibility/performance/fallback for advanced media.

For such a move, determine:
`decision -> affected protected constraints -> evidence/authority basis -> eligible | transform | reject | escalate`.

An ineligible move must not be carried forward as a preferred concept with a warning attached. Transform or remove it before creative comparison.

This is not a checklist for every color, spacing, or typography micro-decision. It is an admission boundary for decisions with material professional consequences.

## J-07 — Proof provenance firewall

Principle: proof-bearing content requires provenance before it can be presented as factual visual content.

For any proposed customer, logo, metric, review, rating, inventory fact, location, guarantee, delivery claim, certification, partner, product feature, product state, or other factual/business proof, classify the source state:
- `VERIFIED_SUPPLIED` — supplied or retrieved from an authoritative project source and applicable to the claim;
- `UNKNOWN_OR_UNVERIFIED` — not established;
- `CONCEPTUAL_NON_PROOF` — clearly fictional/abstract and cannot reasonably be mistaken for real business/product evidence.

Only `VERIFIED_SUPPLIED` may be presented as factual proof.

If the state is `UNKNOWN_OR_UNVERIFIED`:
- do not invent the content;
- do not write a plausible replacement value;
- do not imply that a placeholder is real;
- reserve structure only when useful and label it `ASSET NEEDED`, `COPY NEEDED`, or `UNVERIFIED — DO NOT PUBLISH`;
- if the composition depends on unavailable proof, redesign the composition so it remains valid without fabricated evidence.

Aesthetic completeness is never grounds to manufacture proof.

## J-08 — Reference abstraction and derivative-distance test

Principle: references supply evidence about mechanisms, not permission to reproduce a recognizable solution.

Before transferring a reference-derived idea, extract:
`source problem -> mechanism/principle -> context-specific expression -> derivative risk -> transformed application`.

A proposed direction is reference-ineligible when its value depends on reproducing a distinctive combination from a reference, such as the same recognizable hero composition, signature interaction, typography treatment, imagery treatment, spatial sequence, or other coupled expression with only superficial substitutions.

When derivative risk is material, transform at least one underlying mechanism or structural relationship rather than merely changing colors, copy, assets, radii, or brand tokens.

Use a practical distance question:
`Would a competent observer recognize the source mainly because the solution preserves its distinctive expression rather than because both solve a similar problem?`

If yes, transform or reject before shortlisting.

Do not repair imitation risk by avoiding references. Strong reference literacy remains required; the output must abstract principles and create an independently justified solution.

## J-09 — Mobile task-equivalence admission

Principle: a direction that materially changes layout, hierarchy, interaction, or advanced-media behavior is incomplete until its narrow-screen transformation preserves the essential task.

Before admitting such a direction, specify enough of the mobile state to verify:
- essential task remains possible;
- primary action remains discoverable, legible, and operable;
- required information is not silently removed;
- focal order/hierarchy is intentional rather than an accidental desktop stack;
- density and type remain usable;
- desktop-only effects have an equivalent fallback or are removed without breaking meaning/function.

If these cannot be shown from the proposed mobile transformation, mark the move `MOBILE INELIGIBLE` and transform it before selection. Do not defer basic mobile viability until after frontend implementation.

Mobile need not visually imitate desktop. Functional and communicative equivalence may use a different composition.

## J-10 — Authority mutation firewall

Principle: distinguish visual representation of an approved interaction from mutation of the interaction itself.

Before a material visual decision that changes sequence, required fields/information, step count, CTA behavior, navigation meaning, disclosure timing, funnel logic, information architecture meaning, product state, or conversion logic is admitted, ask whether the approved upstream contract already authorizes that behavior.

If not explicitly authorized:
- classify `UPSTREAM_CONSTRAINT`;
- preserve the current approved behavior in the visual contract;
- phrase the alternative as a recommendation/question for the responsible owner, not as an implemented design decision;
- continue with an in-authority visual solution when possible.

A cleaner composition, stronger hierarchy, higher conversion hypothesis, or more dramatic interaction is not authorization.

## J-11 — Gate evidence, not gate assertion

The existing FUNCTION/MOBILE/AUTHORITY/TRUTH/ADVANCED-MEDIA gates remain mandatory, but a PASS label must be supported by the decision evidence created during the work.

A gate is not passed by writing `PASS` after an incompatible decision. If a material move lacks the required evidence, provenance, mobile transformation, or authority basis, the corresponding gate is unresolved or failed.

Ready-state evaluation therefore verifies that no ineligible material decision survived admission control.

## Non-regression requirements

Preserve all previously demonstrated strengths:
- genuine divergence before convergence;
- current reference research and benchmark extraction;
- reference literacy without blanket avoidance;
- concept distinctiveness and strong craft judgment;
- justified creative risk and rule-breaking within hard constraints;
- willingness to route motion/3D/WebGL when it performs a real job and survives feasibility gates;
- rendered critique/root-cause diagnosis;
- visual recommendation of upstream opportunities without unauthorized implementation;
- ability to use clearly conceptual non-proof imagery when project rules permit it.

A repair that becomes generic, timid, anti-reference, anti-3D, or unable to recommend upstream improvement is a regression.
