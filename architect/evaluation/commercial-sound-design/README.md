# Commercial Sound Design candidate EXTEND evaluation

Status: development/narrow evaluation design for issue #289. Not qualification evidence until executed under a frozen cycle with independent evaluation and the real-media practical gate.

## Scope

Evaluate only the new Commercial Sound Design capability and material interactions with the unchanged qualified parent:
- sonic concept/hierarchy;
- source/fit/truth;
- layering;
- transitions;
- silence;
- sound-driven macro rhythm;
- creative mix judgment;
- artifact-first real-media perceptual QC.

Do not rerun unrelated parent VE competencies unless a failure suggests shared coupling.

## Evaluation layers

1. **Deterministic contract**
   - candidate references exact qualified parent digest;
   - status remains candidate/not-qualified;
   - cases are unique and contain forbidden advancement conditions;
   - real-media PASS cannot be inferred from semantic output.

2. **Development semantic/adversarial gate**
   - visible cases in `semantic-cases.json`;
   - target root-cause and judgment failures, not vocabulary recall;
   - max three provider calls per invocation.

3. **Independent holdout**
   - fresh cases must not be copied into candidate instructions;
   - include random-SFX temptation, silence aversion, misleading product sound, technically-correct-but-flat mix and perspective mismatch.

4. **Real-media practical gate**
   - actual rendered short-form commercial;
   - full audiovisual listen, not timeline/log inference;
   - calibrated subjective review for coherence, craft, commercial function and truth;
   - declared playback contexts;
   - exact artifact identity/hash.

5. **Applied automotive gate**
   - authoritative specialization and Toyota Yaris AM-001 test remain in `auto-sales-growth-system`.

## Critical release criteria

P0:
- no materially false product/event sound;
- no READY/PASS claim without actual produced-artifact perceptual inspection.

P1:
- coherent sonic concept and event hierarchy;
- intentional layering/negative space/transitions;
- macro rhythm/emotional arc without relying on music;
- final mix survives realistic playback;
- reviewers do not perceive the result as random SFX decoration.

A high semantic score cannot compensate for a failed real-media craft gate.

## Stop-loss

Follow `architect/methodology/qualification-stop-loss.md`. Infrastructure failure is not professional PASS/FAIL. Preserve valid completed evidence and stop bounded chains as required.
