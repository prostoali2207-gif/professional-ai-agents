# Note-Taking — evaluation plan v0.1

Status: PRE-SKILL PLAN
Date: 2026-09-23

## Claim boundary

Candidate claim: the skill can analyze and structure note-taking decisions across lesson types, route format from information structure, preserve exactness where required, and provide a domain task model for a separate coaching layer.

It does not claim a universal note format or teaching sequence.

## Critical claims

### NT-C1 — Selection without semantic loss
Separates core proposition from support/repetition while preserving reconstructability.

### NT-C2 — Structure recognition
Identifies hierarchy, comparison, process, or relation before choosing format.

Hard fail: format chosen by habit rather than material structure.

### NT-C3 — Exactness control
Preserves or flags exact-source material; does not present uncertain reconstruction as quotation.

### NT-C4 — Compression quality
Compression removes surface wording without deleting conditions, exceptions, causal relations, or required support.

### NT-C5 — Retrieval utility
Notes can support reconstruction of main ideas/relations after the source is hidden.

### NT-C6 — Islamic source fidelity
When Islamic specialization is active, source/authority layers remain distinguishable; material khilaf/tarjih/conditions are not flattened.

### NT-C7 — Anti-template behavior
No fixed Cornell/three-bullet/mind-map/default structure is imposed across structurally different lessons.

### NT-C8 — Composition
When the objective is learning, pedagogy is delegated to `learning-coach`; note-taking remains the domain owner.

## Evaluation families

1. verbose hierarchical lecture;
2. repeated comparison dimensions;
3. procedure with branches/exceptions;
4. exact definition + explanatory paraphrase;
5. notes that are short but lose meaning;
6. live vs recorded constraints;
7. Islamic lesson with Qur'an/hadith/scholar/teacher layers;
8. Islamic disagreement/conditions case;
9. request for "best universal method";
10. coaching request that must compose with learning-coach.

## Grading

Use proposition/relationship reference maps where deterministic.
For fidelity cases, required-source labels and preserved conditions can be checked explicitly.
Format appropriateness and compression quality require independent semantic review for T1.

## Release status

Author-side static/development checks may support project-use candidate status only.
T1 requires independent held-out practical evaluation.
