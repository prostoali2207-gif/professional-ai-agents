Status: research pool for issue #312. This is not yet the final frozen scored set.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer:
1. official platform case studies with downstream outcomes;
2. public account/reel evidence with observable performance;
3. curated dealer-specific reel libraries;
4. practitioner articles only for mechanism hypotheses.

Third-party Instagram mirrors are discovery/measurement aids, not authoritative platform analytics. Treat their metrics as public observational evidence with possible measurement error.

## Observed market mechanisms worth testing

These are hypotheses from the source pool, not rules:

- hook in the first ~2 seconds;
- one clear idea per reel;
- short duration / fast payoff;
- price or budget anchoring;
- comparison/choice;
- buyer problem or negotiation POV;
- simple physical test/demonstration;
- hidden feature / useful information;
- real salesperson/personality;
- challenge/game/guessing;
- ASMR/sensory demonstration;
- trust/condition transparency;
- direct next step when sales intent is high.

## Specific anti-bias rules

- Do not reward a reference because it is viral.
- Do not assume a reference is professionally strong because it is from a famous showroom.
- Do not treat follower-normalized breakout as causal proof.
- Do not treat paid-campaign outcomes as creative-only outcomes.
- Do not copy public scripts or branded concepts into hidden tasks.
- Do not infer that a music-driven reference is unusable; extract the non-music mechanism and test whether it survives.
- Do not punish the candidate for preserving truthful claims where a market reference uses unsupported hype.

## Final-set requirements before scoring

The frozen scored set should contain:
- multiple ordinary/used-car references;
- multiple low-production concepts;
- UAE/GCC cases;
- at least one performance/outcome-supported case;
- at least one viral outlier;
- at least one ordinary/weak control;
- at least one comparison/test format;
- at least one POV/humor or buyer-tension format;
- at least one utility/trust format;
- no single account dominating the set.

Final references must be archived by URL/source/date/observable metrics and tagged for confounders before candidate generation.
