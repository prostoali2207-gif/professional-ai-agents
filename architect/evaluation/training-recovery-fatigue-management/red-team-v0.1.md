# Training Recovery & Fatigue Management — pre-SKILL red-team v0.1

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: completed before candidate SKILL.

Question:
What would a strong practitioner notice is missing even if the user did not ask for it?

## Senior strength-and-conditioning practitioner

Finding: fatigue cannot be interpreted without exercise comparability and recent programming changes.
Repair: added comparable-performance anchors, exercise order/ROM/equipment context, novelty and intervention history.

Finding: deload is often used as a vague catch-all.
Repair: candidate must compare targeted set/failure/frequency adjustment before full deload and preserve stimulus.

## Sport scientist / measurement specialist

Finding: a multivariate system can still become pseudo-science if it collapses signals into an unexplained score.
Repair: no composite recovery score is required or authoritative; evidence streams remain inspectable with provenance.

Finding: device data are method-dependent.
Repair: store device/model/source; prefer within-person trends; prohibit exact sleep-stage/CNS/hormonal inference.

## Educator / competency assessor

Finding: labels such as systemic fatigue are not observable competencies.
Repair: RF-04 requires repeated performance degradation plus broader recovery strain and case-based evidence. The label is explicitly operational/non-clinical.

Finding: plateau and under-recovery can be confused.
Repair: plateau requires recovery/adherence checks before concluding stimulus mismatch.

## Hiring-manager perspective

Finding: a competent practitioner should learn whether previous changes worked.
Repair: intervention ledger is mandatory and prior outcomes must be consulted before repeating a change.

Finding: changing several training variables at once destroys attribution.
Repair: smallest-sufficient-change rule unless severity or logistics require otherwise.

## Evaluation scientist

Finding: one-day and wearable-score traps need explicit adversarial cases.
Repair: both become hard-fail families.

Finding: development case success cannot equal release qualification.
Repair: readiness ceiling remains CANDIDATE / NOT QUALIFIED until held-out behavioral and practical evidence executes.

## Sports-medicine boundary

Finding: OTS/NFOR are not appropriate self-diagnoses; persistent unexplained deterioration can have non-training causes.
Repair: medical diagnosis is out of scope; severe/persistent/unexplained health concerns escalate.

## Systems/state perspective

Finding: compaction could erase the evidence behind a program change.
Repair: comparable anchors, anomalies and intervention ledger are non-discardable state.

Finding: missing values may be normalized accidentally.
Repair: explicit missing != normal rule.

## Red-team result

PASS FOR PRE-SKILL GATE after repairs above.

No new generic framework or separate recovery agent is justified.
