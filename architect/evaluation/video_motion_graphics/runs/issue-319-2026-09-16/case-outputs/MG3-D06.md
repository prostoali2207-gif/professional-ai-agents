# MG3-D06 — Selected direction exceeds renderer's asset-authoring capability

## Decision

This is a **runtime/asset-authoring route** problem (MG3-14 / MG-09), not a craft problem and not a brief problem. The renderer is eligible as an animation and composition surface; it is not sufficient to author the assets the selected direction depends on. I do not proceed to full implementation on that surface, and I do not quietly re-skin the direction as animated text and CSS shapes.

Two routes are legitimate, and I pick one explicitly with the client rather than drifting into the third:

1. **Route the asset authoring out.** Custom vector illustration, textured masks and a hand-built icon set are produced in an eligible authoring capability (vector/illustration tool, texture/matte generation, an illustrator), exported as SVG/PNG-with-alpha/pre-rendered mattes at delivery resolution plus headroom, and imported into the renderer, which then does what it is actually good at: layout, keyframing, easing, masking and compositing of supplied assets.
2. **Deliberately re-select a simpler direction** that the available surface can author honestly and that still meets the brief — typically a type-led or geometric-system direction with authored composition, scale and timing rather than illustration-dependent surface.

The forbidden third route is the silent downgrade: keeping the approved direction's name while shipping generic cards, default icon fonts, gradient fills and CSS filters standing in for texture. That is `GENERIC_VISUAL_SYSTEM` plus a false craft claim, and under MG3-12 it lands as `CRAFT_FAIL` at phone-size review even if every technical check passes.

## Reasoning

- MG3-14 requires separating renderer capability, asset-authoring capability and professional craft. Here the first is present, the second is absent, and the third cannot be claimed on the basis of the first.
- The direction was selected comparatively (MG3-02) against the brief; its distinctiveness lives specifically in the illustration, the texture and the icon language. Strip those and what remains is not a cheaper version of the same direction — it is a different, weaker one that was never compared or approved. Re-selection must therefore be a stated decision, not an implementation side effect.
- MG3-13 stops this before cost accrues: the prototype gate exists precisely so an unauthorable direction is caught at styleframe/prototype, not after full-length animation.

## Assumptions stated

Structural edit is locked (MG3-03), the beat map and overlay ledger are unaffected, no locked layer is implicated (MG3-10), and the delivery target is vertical mobile social. If the illustration set is client-supplied brand property, Route 1 is a handoff request, not new authoring.

## Concrete actions

1. Write a capability gap note: required asset classes (vector illustration set, textured masks/mattes, icon family — count, states, resolutions), what the renderer can and cannot author, and the evidence for that judgment.
2. Put both routes to the direction owner with cost/time and craft consequences, and a recommendation: Route 1 if the illustration budget and turnaround exist, Route 2 if not.
3. If Route 1: define the asset spec (artboard sizes, safe areas, alpha requirements, naming, layer separation for animatable parts, colour space), then prototype the motion language on placeholder assets so timing review is not blocked.
4. If Route 2: run a fresh short divergence on directions authorable in-surface, produce three styleframes (opening / peak explanatory / resolved) plus one animated prototype, and get re-selection recorded.
5. Do not schedule a render or make any craft claim until the chosen route is approved.
