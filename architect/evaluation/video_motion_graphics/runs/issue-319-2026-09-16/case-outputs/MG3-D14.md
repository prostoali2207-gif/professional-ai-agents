## Decision

I will add the diagram and leave the lower-third package exactly as approved — same position, same scale, same timing, same style. The convenience of moving it is not a reason that outranks the lock. The diagram gets designed around the package, not the package around the diagram.

## Diagnosis

Two separate reasons, and it matters that they are separate.

First, the lock itself. The package is `LOCKED_BY_APPROVAL`-grade at minimum and explicitly `LOCKED_BY_EXPERIMENT`, which makes it an invariant for this revision. Moving or resizing it silently is `LOCKED_LAYER_DRIFT`, regardless of whether the result looks better.

Second, and more expensive: a `LOCKED_BY_EXPERIMENT` layer is not only protected, it is likely part of the measured variable or held constant against it. Changing its position or scale is variant drift outside the tested variable. That does not just alter this cut — it invalidates the running test and whatever spend and read time sit behind it. The request as written ("add the diagram only") is also explicitly scoped to the diagram. Moving the package would be me widening my own authority.

Assumptions I am proceeding on, stated rather than asked: the experiment is live or its results are not yet read; the diagram brief and its underlying meaning are already approved; the structural edit is stable enough to author motion against, and if it is not, the diagram waits for structural lock (MG3-03) rather than being animated into a moving target.

## Actions

1. **Lock map first.** Record the lower-third package as `LOCKED_BY_EXPERIMENT`, with its exact on-screen region, safe margins and in/out time ranges. Everything downstream treats that rectangle and those time ranges as unavailable geometry.
2. **Solve around the lock.** The usable design space is the frame minus the locked region minus subject/proof and caption territory. Three routes, in order of preference:
   - **Temporal separation** — place or peak the diagram in the windows where the package is not on screen. Cheapest and cleanest; costs nothing in composition.
   - **Spatial separation** — the diagram lives in the remaining upper/centre field with honest margins against the package, no encroachment, no overlap during its live range.
   - **Compositional mode change** (MG-07) — split composition, or a temporary full-frame explanation placed in a package-free window. Least complex mode that still reads wins.
3. **Choose a mechanism that fits the available space, not one that demands the space be cleared.** Per MG3-08, I test persistent-object state change, transfer/migration and relationship-change representations before defaulting to numbered cards or `01 → 02 → 03`. A vertically compact persistent-object or transfer mechanism is likely to fit the residual field far better than a card row, and is usually the truer representation anyway. No implied magnitudes, rates or timestamps without approved evidence.
4. **Styleframes with the locked package composited in.** Opening, peak explanatory and resolved states must be judged with the lower-third present. Judging the diagram on a clean frame would be self-deception. If hierarchy fails statically, it does not get animated.
5. **Overlay ledger.** The diagram's labels get entries: text → approved semantic statement/source beat → time range → owner/evidence. Nothing unsupported ships.
6. **Motion gain check.** Each animated element declares job, represented change, viewer inference, timing/hold, removal criterion. Combined attention load is judged against the package, not in isolation.
7. **Integrate, render from the controlled source path, then technical QC and craft QC at phone size** — including a diff check that the locked package is untouched.

## If it genuinely will not fit

Then I do not quietly compromise. I report the exact dependency — which diagram mechanism, in which time range, needs which specific pixels — and the *minimum* unlock required, and route it to the experiment owner as `NEEDS_UPSTREAM_REVISION`. Their call, not mine.
