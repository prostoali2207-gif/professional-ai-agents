# MG3-D09 — Caption emphasis policy

## Decision

Enhanced caption emphasis is approved as a *licensed device*, not as a house style. "Successful reels do it" is a reference observation, not a justification — under the reference gate it may tell us a mechanism exists, it cannot become the default surface template. So: no blanket grow/tilt/colour pass over the caption track. Each accent must be individually licensed against the test below, and captions stay the third layer behind subject/proof and the explanatory graphic.

## Reasoning

Three layers are competing for the same attention budget: the subject, the explanatory graphic, and the captions. Accent applied per-word to a track that is already running continuously does not add emphasis — it raises the floor. Once every third word grows, tilts or turns yellow, the viewer has no emphasis left to read, and the two layers that actually carry the argument (the subject's face beats and the diagram's peak explanatory state) lose their turn. That is the observable failure, not a taste objection.

Tilt is the weakest of the three requested devices: it carries no semantic mapping, enlarges the bounding box (collision risk against face, proof and CTA), and is the single strongest generic-template signal of the set. Growth and colour can both be mapped to meaning and are the ones I would author.

## Emphasis licence test (all must hold per accent)

1. **Semantic load** — the word is the load-bearing element of its beat: the changed state, the contrast term, the named object, the approved figure, the CTA verb. Function words, connectives, filler and repeats are never eligible.
2. **Stated inference** — I can write `job -> represented change -> intended viewer inference -> timing/hold -> removal criterion` for it. If I cannot, it is decoration and it goes.
3. **Consistent device mapping** — one device per function, held for the whole piece: weight/scale = importance; one accent colour = exactly one semantic category (e.g. the "new" side of the old→new contrast, borrowed from the diagram's own palette role); position/mask/kinetic = arrival timing only. No device does two jobs.
4. **Hierarchy window** — no accent inside the diagram's peak explanatory window or on a subject's proof beat, unless the accent *is* the diagram's own label. Captions yield there.
5. **Budget** — at most one accented unit per caption group, and roughly one accent per 3–4 seconds across the piece. Combined attention load is judged, not each effect alone.
6. **Truth boundary (parent VE-07)** — accents cannot alter approved wording, and cannot manufacture a claim by emphasis: no colour or scale on an unevidenced number, magnitude, rate or superlative.
7. **Technical safety** — real weights/italics preflighted (no synthesised faux styles), glyph coverage confirmed, contrast held at phone size over the actual footage, grown/tilted bounding box inside safe area with no face/proof/CTA collision.
8. **Timing** — accent lands on its own syllable, holds long enough to read, and returns to base state before the next accent. No accumulating residual states.

## Actions

- Extend the overlay ledger with an accent column: `word -> approved semantic statement -> device -> time range -> job/inference -> removal`. Unlicensed accents do not reach render.
- Mark candidates off the semantic beat map; I expect one to two per 30s beat cluster, not per sentence.
- Author two comparative treatments against the same brief: (A) restrained — weight/scale on load-bearing terms plus a single mapped accent colour; (B) kinetic — adds mask reveal and position shift. Compare on communication, coherence with the graphic, typography craft, timing quality, restraint, mobile final impression. Both preserved as legitimate alternatives.
- Gate: styleframes at opening / peak explanatory / resolved state, including one frame with captions live over the diagram's peak, plus one short animated prototype of the accent language. Nothing rolls out full-length before that passes.
- If the caption layer is `LOCKED_BY_USER` or `LOCKED_BY_APPROVAL`, I do not restyle it; I report the dependency and the minimum unlock required.
