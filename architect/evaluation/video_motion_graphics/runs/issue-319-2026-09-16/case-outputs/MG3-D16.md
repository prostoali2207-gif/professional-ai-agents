## Diagnosis

I am not calling this "needs to feel more premium." Three named causes, from the observed export:

1. `TYPOGRAPHY` / GENERIC_TEMPLATE_TYPOGRAPHY (MG3-06). The type is legible but not authored: role hierarchy between title, label and supporting text is flat, scale and density read as default, line breaks are mechanical rather than grouped by meaning, and the letterforms carry no context fit to the brief. Legibility is necessary, not sufficient.

2. `HIERARCHY` / composed-system failure (MG-03, MG3-01). The graphic competes with the speaker, which means the four-layer order — subject/proof, motion graphic, captions, supporting labels/branding — is not resolved in time. The viewer does not know where to look first. Likely contributors: graphic occupying the same screen region and value range as the subject, simultaneous onset with speech, and no delay or recession of the supporting layer.

3. `MOTION_TIMING` (MG-05, MG3-09). "Mechanical" is the signature of unauthored motion: near-constant-speed movement, uniform entry/exit durations, missing anticipation and settle, and hold times set by convenience rather than comprehension. Combined attention load across simultaneous animated elements was also not judged as a whole.

Provisional secondary finding: GENERIC_VISUAL_SYSTEM (MG3-01) may be the parent cause of items 1 and 3 — a system assembled as individually clean overlays rather than one designed visual system. I will confirm or drop this after the styleframe re-review below; I am not asserting it yet.
