# MG3-D15 — Release-master source path

## Decision

Release master conforms to **original camera media**. The deliberate high-quality intermediate is retained as an authorized mezzanine and may carry the master only for elements that genuinely originate in it. The **compressed review export is disqualified** as a master source, and the low-resolution proxies are editorial-only and are relinked away before mastering.

Ranking of the four available paths:

1. Original camera media — master path.
2. Deliberate high-quality intermediate — eligible mezzanine; acceptable as master source for ranges where it *is* the authored origin (e.g. a graded/flattened comp or a render whose upstream elements no longer reconform cleanly), provided its own lineage to camera originals is documented and it is visually-lossless at or above delivery resolution/bit depth.
3. Low-resolution proxies — editorial and prototype only. Never a master source.
4. Compressed review export — never a master source. Mastering from it is an avoidable lossy generation chain under MG3-11.

## Reasoning

MG3-11 permits professional proxies and a deliberate mastering-grade intermediate, and forbids deriving a release master from review exports, social downloads, proxies or accidental lossy chains **when an approved higher-quality path exists**. It does exist here: the camera originals. So there is no defensible justification for the review export, and using the proxies would bake proxy resolution, codec and any proxy-side transform into the deliverable. The intermediate is legitimate, but it is a second generation; I only spend that generation where it buys something real (a committed grade or a comp that cannot be rebuilt identically), not across the whole programme by default.

Because editing happened on proxies, the risk is not only resolution. Motion graphics authored against proxy dimensions can carry pixel-unit positions, font sizes, stroke widths, blur radii and mask/track data tied to the proxy raster, and proxies frequently carry a different color transform than the camera originals. Those are the failure modes I check, not just "is it 4K now".

## Actions

1. Verify camera-original identity and rights: checksums, frame counts, timecode and duration matched against the proxy set clip-for-clip; log any clip with no valid original and raise it as `MISSING_OR_INVALID_ASSET` rather than substituting the review export for that range.
2. Relink/conform the locked timeline from proxies to camera originals. Confirm no frame offsets, no reversed speed changes, no dropped handles.
3. Re-establish color management explicitly: input transform of the camera originals, working space, output transform for the delivery target. Confirm corrective work and any creative look survive the relink and were not tuned against a proxy transform.
4. Re-render motion graphics and typography at master resolution and bit depth from source assets (vector/text/comp), not by upscaling proxy-era renders. Preflight fonts/glyphs again; re-verify safe placement, line breaks, scale and stroke weight at master size, then at phone size.
5. Re-validate tracking, masks and mattes against full-resolution plates — proxy-derived track data is a known drift point.
6. Record lineage: `camera original -> conformed timeline -> motion assets/render -> (mezzanine where used) -> master`, with every material transcode and its settings.
7. Export master to the validated destination spec, then run technical QC on the decoded artifact, then phone-size craft QC on the same artifact.

## Assumptions stated

Camera originals are accessible, complete and rights-cleared; the intermediate's provenance is documentable; the structural edit is locked; the overlay ledger is unchanged by this decision.
