## Diagnosis

This is not a craft defect. It is a semantic overlay integrity failure (MG3-04, overlay ledger) with a truth/provenance edge (parent VE-04/VE-07).

Approved narration describes a process that continues through three phases: purchase, holding, later exit. The overlay asserts the cycle terminates at purchase. That is not a paraphrase, a compression, or a stylistic emphasis choice — it removes two phases of the approved meaning and states the opposite endpoint. Overlay text is read faster and remembered longer than narration, and on mute (the dominant social viewing condition) the overlay *is* the claim. So the composed system currently communicates a falsehood against its own approved source.

Defect classification: `TEXT_GRAPHICS` primary, `TRUTH_OR_PROVENANCE` secondary risk (the overlay could be taken as a factual statement about the process), `CONCEPT` if the mechanism behind the overlay was built around a terminal-at-purchase structure rather than a continuing lifecycle. Craft classes (`TYPOGRAPHY`, `MOTION_TIMING`, `COMPOSITING`) are clean and I am not touching them.

Explicit rule applied: a beautiful overlay that contradicts narration or brief is a composed-system failure even when typography and animation are flawless. There is no release-ready status until the contradictory overlay is repaired and re-observed.

## Assumptions

1. The narration is the approved semantic source; the overlay carries no separate approval evidence that supersedes it.
2. The overlay layer is `UNLOCKED`. If the lock map shows `LOCKED_BY_USER`, `LOCKED_BY_APPROVAL` or `LOCKED_BY_EXPERIMENT` on that layer, I do not edit it — I report the exact dependency and the minimum unlock required (this one text layer, this one time range) and the hold stands until unlock.
3. I do not own claims. I can correct an overlay back into agreement with already-approved meaning; I cannot author a new assertion about the process.

## Actions

1. Freeze release. No master export, no handoff, no delivery of this cut for publication.
2. Complete the overlay ledger for every material overlay in the piece, not just this one: `overlay text -> approved semantic statement/source beat -> time range -> owner/evidence`. A contradiction this size usually indicates the ledger was never run, so I expect to find siblings.
3. Locate the origin: earlier script version, a copy-paste from another deliverable, or a mechanism decision (a "cycle ends here" visual) that pulled the wording with it. Repair the responsible layer, not the symptom.
4. Propose bounded replacement wording that maps to the approved narration beat and keeps the existing type and motion system intact — e.g. a phase label at the purchase beat rather than a terminal statement, so the overlay names where we are instead of declaring the end. Route the wording to the brief/fact owner for confirmation before I commit it.
5. Check the mechanism (MG3-08). If the graphic is built as a terminating sequence, the diagram itself contradicts the narration and needs the persistent-object-through-states or transfer/hold/exit representation instead. That is a styleframe-level fix, not a text swap.
6. After repair, re-run affected checks: parent caption/text QC, decode, glyph and safe-placement, then re-observe at representative phone size with sound off and on. Only then can craft be judged and a `PRACTICAL_PASS_CANDIDATE` even be considered.
