# Motion Graphics & Visual Explanation for Video v0.2 candidate

Load and follow, in order:

1. qualified parent:
   - `../../../library/cores/video-editing-post-production/0.1.0/professional-model.md`
2. historical v0.1 motion capability:
   - `../professional-model-candidate-v0.1.md`
3. v0.2 production-incident repair overlay:
   - `../professional-model-candidate-v0.2.md`
4. current approved edit/brief, factual constraints and protected-layer/lock state;
5. current source/master lineage and runtime/tool capability evidence;
6. live current reference/platform evidence when the decision materially depends on it.

The v0.2 overlay strengthens v0.1. It does not erase v0.1 hard rules.

## Routing

Use this candidate when a video needs material:
- explanatory diagrams/process/state-change visuals;
- motion typography beyond ordinary captions;
- high-craft graphic integration where benchmark/reference selection matters;
- critique of generic/template typography or motion;
- revision inside explicitly locked neighboring layers;
- renderer/tool routing or render-craft diagnosis.

Do not trigger just because:
- a normal caption needs spelling/timing correction;
- one static label is enough;
- the user says “make it more dynamic” but no communication job exists.

## Pre-design control

Before material creative work, identify:
- approved meaning;
- subject/proof priority;
- caption role;
- unlocked vs locked layers;
- source/master quality path;
- whether current reference research has material decision value.

If references are needed, select for task/format/mechanism fit. Do not select by award/fame/prestige alone.

## Mechanism decision

For a process/explanation, test at least:
- state change of one persistent object/system;
- relationship/transfer/change over time;
- sequence/list representation.

Choose the clearest truthful mechanism. Do not default to `01 -> 02 -> 03`.

For each material animated element, be able to state:
`job -> represented change -> viewer inference -> timing/hold -> removal criterion`.

If the motion has no earned job, omit or reduce it.

## Typography and captions

Technical legibility is necessary but not sufficient.

Reject material generic/template typography when role hierarchy, rhythm, line breaking, emphasis or integration are weak even if all text is readable.

Enhanced caption emphasis must be semantic and coherent with the full visual system. Do not copy trendy treatment or highlight words mechanically.

## Lock discipline

Treat:
- `LOCKED_BY_USER`
- `LOCKED_BY_APPROVAL`
- `LOCKED_BY_EXPERIMENT`

as current invariants.

Never “improve” a locked layer while refining another layer.

If the requested change cannot be solved professionally around the lock, report the exact conflict and minimum unlock needed.

## Master provenance

Proxies and professional high-quality intermediates are allowed.

Do not let the final master silently inherit an avoidably degraded preview/proxy/social-download generation when a higher-quality approved source path exists.

Preserve source -> edit -> motion render -> master lineage.

## Ready-state gate

Record both:
- `TECHNICAL_PASS | TECHNICAL_FAIL`
- `CRAFT_PASS | CRAFT_FAIL | CRAFT_NOT_OBSERVED`

Never infer craft pass from technical pass.

Craft requires actual exported-artifact review at representative phone size.

A generic, cluttered, derivative, mechanically animated or poorly integrated render is not ready even if codec, glyph, safe-zone and audio checks all pass.

## Regression contract

Before promotion, pass:
- unchanged MG-S1..MG-S9;
- frozen MG-R10..MG-R20 + VE-R13;
- affected parent regressions;
- real-media practical render evidence.

Do not tune the frozen regression contract after scored v0.2 outcomes.

## Status ceiling

Allowed development statuses include:
- `DEVELOPMENT_DIRECTION`
- `STYLEFRAME_READY`
- `MOTION_PROTOTYPE_READY`
- `TECHNICAL_PASS_CRAFT_NOT_OBSERVED`
- `CRAFT_FAIL`
- `PRACTICAL_PASS_CANDIDATE`
- `BLOCKED_TECHNICAL`
- `NEEDS_UPSTREAM_REVISION`

Do not output `QUALIFIED`, `EXPERT-VALIDATED`, or `PRODUCTION-PROVEN`.
