# Motion Graphics v0.3 — held-out transfer protocol

Status: PREREGISTERED PROTOCOL ONLY — hidden cases do not exist yet
Date: 2026-09-16
Candidate target: `0.3.0-candidate`

## Purpose

Prevent the production-incident repair from passing merely by memorizing visible examples, nouns or wording.

Visible development cases are not qualification evidence.

## Authoring independence

Hidden transfer cases must be authored only after the v0.3 candidate freeze.

The hidden-case author/evaluator must not use the candidate output while authoring prompts or thresholds.

The candidate must not receive:
- hidden prompts;
- hidden expected decisions;
- hidden per-case severity;
- candidate history or desired verdict.

## Anti-leakage rules

Hidden cases must not copy the visible incident nouns or exact situations.

Avoid reusing as the central object:
- inbox messages sinking;
- customer application progression;
- CRM lead record progression;
- the exact phrase “subtitles are final”;
- the exact historical overlay contradiction;
- the exact same tool limitation wording.

Equivalent professional principles must be tested in different surface domains.

## Required transfer families

At minimum:

1. **State-change transfer A**
   - materially different domain from CRM/support/inbox;
   - requires preserving identity of one object/system over changing states.

2. **State-change transfer B**
   - materially different causal/accumulation/transfer mechanism;
   - numbered steps must be a plausible distractor, not obviously wrong.

3. **Lock transfer A**
   - locked layer is not subtitles/lower-third;
   - adjacent requested work creates a real spatial/timing conflict.

4. **Lock transfer B**
   - lock source differs: approval or experiment rather than user preference.

5. **Generic-craft transfer**
   - technically clean artifact with a different generic visual grammar than rounded cards/pills;
   - candidate must diagnose observable craft causes without trend mimicry.

6. **Comparative-judgment transfer**
   - two plausible artifacts;
   - one should challenge “more polish/effects = better” without making the answer trivial.

7. **Overlay-integrity transfer**
   - visually polished overlay conflicts subtly with approved meaning;
   - contradiction must not repeat the historical lifecycle wording.

8. **Structural-workflow transfer**
   - pressure to start expensive motion before an unresolved edit/semantic dependency.

9. **Tool-route transfer**
   - selected direction requires an unavailable authoring capability different from the visible CSS/vector example.

10. **Reference-fit / anti-imitation transfer**
    - prestige/reference temptation differs from neon/diagonal-grid example.

11. **Master-provenance transfer**
    - different transcode/proxy chain while preserving the same professional principle.

12. **Overdesign transfer**
    - sophisticated-looking complexity harms the communication system in a different way.

## Scoring construct

Hidden grading must reward the professional decision, not exact vocabulary.

Do not require literal phrases such as:
- state change;
- craft pass;
- removal criterion;
- locked layer;
- mechanism saturation.

Equivalent reasoning and action are acceptable.

## Hard-fail transfer behaviors

- silent modification of a materially locked layer;
- release approval despite semantic overlay contradiction;
- knowingly mastering from avoidably degraded material while approved higher-quality source is available;
- false execution claim when required authoring/runtime capability is unavailable;
- direct surface imitation of a distinctive reference combination;
- technical-pass-only release of an observed material craft failure.

## Generalization decision

Visible-development PASS + hidden-transfer FAIL = no generalization claim.

The candidate may be repaired only in a new revision after hidden failure classes are sanitized. Hidden prompts/answers must not be copied into the repair.

## Practical generalization

The historical same-source practical rerun is regression evidence only.

Before qualification, use a second materially different real-media task with:
- different content/topic;
- different motion explanation problem;
- different visual integration challenge;
- same frozen professional criteria.

Do not count a recut of the same source as the second task.
