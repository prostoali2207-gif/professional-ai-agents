# Conversion Messaging & Web Copy — professional delta v0.2

Status: Architect research artifact. This is not qualification evidence and does not authorize a release verdict.

Issue: #259

## Baseline

Frozen predecessor: `conversion-messaging-web-copy/0.1.0` at commit `7019f6717b1b61806f4a221a297d049a4ad3b8cb`, artifact digest `sha256:da7662f95dcf132d9a9875849b7bb5d5d831d1d54821f0b109b543a1f299e1d2`.

The v0.1 qualification terminated `NOT_EXECUTABLE` under the repository stop-loss. No professional failure was observed. Therefore v0.2 is justified only by independently evidenced professional delta, not by a desire to reset qualification.

## Reuse decision

Overall decision: `EXTEND`.

The stable v0.1 profession model remains valid for evidence calibration, customer-language provenance, message hierarchy, proof/objection matching, genuine framing divergence, exact-copy craft, experiment discipline, upstream escalation, and authority boundaries. No evidence supports rebuilding those mechanisms.

Three material gaps justify extension:

### D1 — Acquisition-source to landing-message continuity

Classification: `EXTEND`, CORE when traffic source/entry promise is known; otherwise contextual.

Observed v0.1 gap: `entry context` is an input, but the candidate has no explicit observable decision contract for continuity between an approved acquisition message/search intent and the landing page. A practitioner can therefore write internally coherent page copy that silently weakens, contradicts, or disconnects the promise/CTA that earned the click.

Required professional behavior:
- identify the source audience/intent, promise/offer, proof basis and expected next action when supplied;
- preserve material continuity from source message to landing message without requiring verbatim repetition;
- detect when the source promise itself exceeds evidence or conflicts with approved proposition/UX state and escalate rather than propagate it;
- avoid optimizing the page in isolation when the approved entry promise creates a materially different expectation;
- distinguish message continuity from acquisition-strategy ownership.

Authority/boundary: Paid Media/CRO owns traffic strategy, targeting, keyword strategy, offer choice and campaign optimization. Conversion Messaging owns wording continuity inside the approved commercial and UX contract.

Observable discriminator: given two entry messages with one material promise/CTA difference and the same product facts, the practitioner adapts page hierarchy/copy only where justified, preserves truth, and flags unsupported source claims rather than copying them.

### D2 — Accessible customer-facing wording inside frozen UX

Classification: `EXTEND`, BOUNDARY-CRITICAL.

Observed v0.1 gap: the `Plain language and accessibility` section is principle-level only. It does not encode testable wording behavior for links, form labels/instructions, and errors even though v0.1 explicitly owns CTA and approved-state microcopy.

Required professional behavior:
- write link/action text whose purpose is understandable in context and distinguishable from unrelated actions;
- provide labels/instructions that tell users what information is expected when wording is part of the supplied UX contract;
- when an input error is detected by the product state, identify the affected item and describe the error in text; where correction guidance is authorized and known, make it actionable rather than generic;
- preserve visible wording clarity for people with cognitive/reading limitations without assuming accessibility is only a frontend concern;
- never invent interaction states or validation rules in order to make microcopy more helpful.

Authority/boundary: UX owns control/state architecture and information requirements; Frontend owns semantic implementation and assistive-technology behavior; Conversion Messaging owns the customer-facing words within those frozen states. Accessibility conformance is a composed-system claim, not something copy alone can certify.

Observable discriminator: when given a frozen form state with vague labels, generic `Something went wrong`, and ambiguous action labels, the practitioner improves wording without changing validation logic, navigation, commitment, or state semantics.

### D3 — Multilingual/localization/translatability judgment

Classification: `EXTEND`, CONTEXTUAL capability activated when more than one language/locale is in scope.

Observed v0.1 gap: it permits live retrieval of current terminology but provides no professional decision model for localization versus literal translation, preservation of evidence strength across languages, translatability, locale-specific wording, or escalation when language competence/evidence is insufficient.

Required professional behavior:
- separate source-language intent from target-language wording;
- preserve proposition, claim strength, proof boundaries, CTA commitment and UX semantics across localized variants;
- avoid idioms, wordplay, concatenated strings or culturally narrow examples when they materially damage translation/localization unless intentionally approved for the target locale;
- distinguish translation from localization; use target-language/customer evidence where available rather than presenting a translation model's output as voice of customer;
- surface locale-dependent formats, directionality or implementation needs as handoff requirements without taking over frontend/i18n architecture;
- require qualified human/local-market review when stakes, language competence, ambiguity or local regulation make self-verification insufficient.

Authority/boundary: Localization specialists/translators and accountable local reviewers own language-quality assurance when required; UX/Frontend own internationalization implementation; Conversion Messaging owns message intent, evidence calibration, translatability and handoff integrity.

Observable discriminator: a source-language persuasive phrase that relies on an idiom or implies stronger certainty after literal translation is rewritten for target meaning, with claim strength and CTA commitment preserved and uncertainty escalated when no valid target-language evidence exists.

## Reused v0.1 competencies

`REUSE` without redesign unless regression shows coupling damage:
- evidence ledger and `VERIFIED | BOUNDED | HYPOTHESIS | UNKNOWN | PROHIBITED` states;
- no-evidence refusal and overall-impression discipline;
- customer-language provenance;
- message architecture and first-comprehension hierarchy;
- genuine divergence versus synonym variation;
- objection/proof matching;
- exact-copy writing and causal critique;
- experiment hypothesis/metric/guardrail/falsifier discipline;
- CRO/User Research/UX/Brand/Legal/Frontend authority boundaries;
- no autonomous publishing;
- live retrieval only when material and source-appropriate.

## Alternatives considered

- `REUSE v0.1 unchanged`: rejected because D1 and D2 are directly relevant to claimed landing-page/customer-facing work and are currently under-specified; D3 is a meaningful recurring web context rather than project trivia.
- `ADAPT only for auto-parts/Spline`: rejected for D1/D2 because they are profession-level across landing pages; D3 remains contextual but reusable across multilingual web work.
- `FORK` or `BUILD NEW`: rejected because most v0.1 professional judgment remains coherent and no evidence supports rebuilding the profession.

## Expert-gap red-team

Senior conversion/content practitioner: would notice that a page can be locally persuasive yet fail the click promise. D1 closes this without moving campaign strategy into copy.

Accessibility/content-design assessor: would reject a generic statement to `be accessible` as non-observable. D2 adds concrete wording behaviors while keeping semantic implementation outside scope.

Acquisition/CRO boundary reviewer: risk is scope creep into keyword/offer/campaign strategy. The delta explicitly accepts approved source intent and escalates unsupported source claims rather than optimizing acquisition.

Localization specialist: risk is treating translation as equivalent to customer language or claiming fluency the agent does not possess. D3 separates localization, target evidence and review/escalation.

Evaluation scientist: each delta produces a counterfactual/adversarial work sample with observable differences; none depends on claimed conversion lift as the release criterion.

## Decision

`V0_2_JUSTIFIED`.

Reason: D1-D3 add material, evidence-backed professional behaviors and evaluation surfaces. They are not infrastructure changes and do not recreate the stopped v0.1 held-out execution chain. A v0.2 candidate may be assembled only after source mapping and evaluation design are frozen as development artifacts.
