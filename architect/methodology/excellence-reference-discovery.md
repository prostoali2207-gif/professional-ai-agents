# Evidence-led Elite Reference Discovery — cross-profession method

Status: operational **candidate procedure**, not a new qualified professional core.
Date: 2026-10-19
Owner: Agent Architect routing; research/provenance executed by the applicable research owner (Market & Competitive Intelligence for market/creative contexts); domain-specific evaluation and decisions owned by the relevant practitioner core.
Related: `../research/creative-reference-intelligence/candidate/SKILL.md`, `professional-core-reuse.md`, `creative-profession-architecture.md`, `practical-use-and-optional-qualification.md`.

## Problem and scope

"Find top references" is **an excellence-discovery problem**, not a search-engine-ranking problem. A name, beautiful thumbnail, viral post, large follower count, award, agency client list, GitHub stars, or impressive case study can generate candidates, but none alone certifies excellence or applicability.

This method works across domains: visual, branding, websites, UX, content, film, ads, software, product, sales, education, operations, research, and others. **Do not impose creative-design measures on noncreative professions**. Define domain-specific observable evidence and who is competent to judge it.

**Output**: a *small, task-specific shortlist of demonstrably strong work*, each anchored to a directly inspected artifact and described honestly in terms of evidence, limits, and transferable mechanism. The ranking is always "best for [dimension / audience / context]", never an unqualified universal top.

## 0. Set the excellence target before searching

Clarify only decision-changing uncertainties. Derive:
- **Decision** the references will improve (e.g. mobile dental landing first screen, service-business Reel hook, CRM case-management state model, lecture-note teaching method).
- **Claim type**: craft/design quality, objective technical quality, usability/task success, market/business outcomes, learning outcomes, novelty/influence, or combination; keep claims separate.
- **Constraints**: audience, budget, geography, device, platform, project scale, accessibility, legal/religious restrictions if relevant, language, time regime, required evidence freshness.
- **Comparable scope**: category, scale/resources, buyer maturity, medium, baseline, intended job to be done. Beware a high-budget global brand as a direct comparator for a small business.
- **Disconfirmers**: what could make an apparently strong reference inappropriate? e.g. clever motion but poor mobile usability, viral attention without qualified leads, prestigious award without access to the real work.

Predefine candidate acceptance in **field-specific observable terms**. Do not use a single universal numerical score or weighted average of incommensurable qualities.

## 1. Build a source map, not a keyword list

Search **independent channels** in parallel or sequentially as resources permit:

| Channel | Find | What the channel can establish | What it cannot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Expert-judged / curated** | discipline-specific awards, transparent jury criteria, reputable curators, historic archives, professional publications | peer judgment of craft/impact on a specified criterion, discovery of makers | business effectiveness, all-field supremacy; submissions/curation are incomplete samples |
| **Creators/operators** | original maker/studio/maintainer, detailed portfolio, repeat commissions, public production and postmortems | actual executed work, recurrence, process and trade-offs | independent causal results unless corroborated |
| **Measurable results** | comparable before/after measurements, A/B tests, task success, retention, credible independently checked field results, engineering benchmarks | outcomes in recorded context; testable mechanisms | transfer to different populations or contexts without revalidation |
| **Expert networks** | recognized specialist peers, jury members, maintainers, conference speakers, credited collaborators, serious critiques/case-study references | high-signal candidates beyond algorithmic popularity | independent validation if everybody repeats the same underlying nomination |
| **Contrast / frontier** | small studios, emerging practitioners, overlooked regions/languages, adjacent domains, informed dissent, high-quality niche archives | recall of excellent less-famous work; new transferable mechanisms | proof of "elite" merely from obscurity or novelty |

**Source-map rules**:
- Start with 2–4 **different mechanisms** of discovery, not five synonyms of "best".
- Prefer primary artifacts; use awards/directories as discovery *pointers*.
- Evaluate curation transparency, scope, entry fees/self-submission, sample coverage, selection bias, and underlying evidence.
- Use field-appropriate native tools. Exa/Tavily/general web discover URLs; official archives/directories discover credited work; platform-native searches inspect video/posts; app/live site/deployed code and user testing verify behavior; analytics only when legally authorized and comparable. Image-search thumbnails and repost galleries are **leads**, not work inspection.
- Search multiple languages/markets where plausible; do not privilege English/US aesthetics as global quality.
- Tools and URLs are capabilities rather than mandatory brand dependencies; if not available, declare the blind spot and seek alternative authoritative paths.

### Practical starting-source examples (verify current availability and criteria live)

- Web/UX: [The Webby Awards criteria](https://www.webbyawards.com/judging-criteria/) and [winner archive](https://winners.webbyawards.com/), followed by **live page/mobile/task inspection**; Nielsen Norman Group research for usability reality checks.
- Advertising/brand/craft: [D&AD judging](https://www.dandad.org/awards/judging) and its award-winning work; inspect the original campaign, context and actual results separately.
- Film/motion: [Vimeo Staff Picks](https://help.vimeo.com/hc/en-us/articles/12426104864017-FAQ-Vimeo-Staff-Picks) as curated leads; watch actual films including pacing/editing, then trace creators.
- Visual project discovery: [Behance Featured Projects](https://help.behance.net/hc/en-us/articles/204483974-Guide-Featured-Projects), then inspect credited projects and usage rather than relying on their cover images.
- Paid short-video ads: [TikTok Top Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-use-the-top-ads-dashboard) permits category/region/objective and available performance-focused filtering. **Authorized showcased ads are an incomplete sample**, and any available numbers require comparable denominators and objectives; distinguish discovery from audited commercial uplift.
- Organic social/Meta ads: inspect original posts / active ad creatives and history where permitted. **Do not infer conversion rate, profitability, qualified leads, or campaign success from public views, ad longevity, or active ads alone.**
- Software/technical/education: use **different authorities**: original repositories and issue history, tests/documentation, independent benchmarks, reproducible task traces, conference demonstrations, curricula or peer-reviewed learner-outcome studies. Do not route technical quality judgment through visual award sites.

This list is a **starter map**, not a universal or exhaustive canon, nor evidence that any listed nominee is best for the current task.

## 2. Traverse the provenance graph

Search paths should produce new **entities and artifacts**, not recycled top-ten lists:

`award / expert review / outcome -> specific project -> original public artifact -> credited maker and collaborators -> 2–3 other relevant works -> peer/jury/competitor graph -> independent corroboration or informed critique`.

Alternative entry: `specific problem -> practitioners with repeated observable solutions -> original examples -> comparable results -> credible peers`.

Work one hop deeper on high-signal leads. Deduplicate the underlying artifact and the underlying source: ten articles citing one award are **one** evidence origin, not ten independent endorsements. Preserve exact URL/author/date and access limits. Search adjacent mechanisms (e.g. documentary editing for a social-services Reel, aviation checklist ergonomics for an operations CRM) but never relabel them **DIRECT**.

## 3. Inspect work and stress-test claims

For every shortlisted candidate, record:

`identity/author -> original exact artifact URL or accessible identifier -> what was actually inspected and on what device/version/date -> goal/context/audience -> excellence dimension -> first-hand observations -> corroborating independent evidence -> recurrence or single canonical exception -> limits/failures -> mechanism -> intended transfer`.

**Inspection depth follows the claim:**
- Website: navigate the live/mobile experience or an adequate authentic recorded walkthrough; test key flow, clarity, performance/accessibility when relevant. A hero screenshot proves only that screenshot.
- Video/social: actually watch the clip and, for a creator-level claim, sample more than one meaningful artifact; analyze hook, beats, legibility, proof, pacing, CTA and context.
- Marketing: separate **creative quality**, exposure and verified **outcomes**; watch for spend, denominator, selection bias, seasonality, audience and attribution.
- Systems/CRM/code: inspect implementation, real state transitions, error handling, security/operability, real-world workflow evidence, and reproducible tests.
- Education: inspect actual sequence/modeling/feedback and credible learner-performance evidence; content that looks polished may teach poorly.
- Historic work: canonical one-off can qualify for a **specific legacy/art-historical claim** if authoritative provenance is strong. Do not invent recurring current performance.

Missing artifact access ⇒ candidate can remain **DISCOVERED / NOT INSPECTED**, never "VERIFIED ELITE".

## 4. Decide who actually clears the elite bar

Use a **multidimensional claim matrix**, not arbitrary aggregate points. For each reference judge:
- **Criterion fit**: elite *at which actual problem/dimension*?
- **Craft and professional judgment**: observable exceptional choices and execution?
- **Evidence strength**: original artifact, domain-competent expert view, independent results, repeatability, source independence?
- **Context transfer**: same audience, constraints, scale and medium, or clearly labelled adjacent mechanism?
- **Risk/limitations**: hidden cost, dependency, usability, copying/rights, selection bias, outdatedness?
- **Distinct contribution**: does it change the downstream decision versus references already admitted?

Admission rule: no serious unresolved evidence/fitness defect; at least one **distinctive demonstrably strong** dimension for the decision; and a first-hand inspected artifact. Repeated excellence is preferred for **creator/studio** rankings; canonical one-off is permitted for **artifact-specific** greatness, without inventing creator consistency.

Evidence labels:
- `CRAFT-EXPERT`: domain-expert/curator credibility for specific craft; outcome unproven.
- `OUTCOME-EVIDENCED`: appropriately sourced contextual outcomes; causal attribution only if justified.
- `REPEATED-OBSERVED`: recurring directly inspected high-quality execution; not equivalent to statistically proved results.
- `CONTEXTUAL-CANDIDATE`: strong potential with limited evidence; not presented as established elite.
- `UNVERIFIED`: metadata/reputation only; excluded from final elite admission.

A source may hold multiple labels. **Do not invent precision**: no global "top 1%" absent valid sampling/measurement and no point scores with unsupported weights. If reliable evidence is scarce, return `PARTIAL` with an explicit blind spot and a constrained next step.

## 5. Build a small, complementary benchmark and hand off

Final benchmark normally has **3–5** task-relevant entries (adapt to task; one exceptional verified source is preferable to five weak ones), divided:
- `DIRECT`: same job/market/scale.
- `ADJACENT_ELITE`: transferable mechanism from exceptional work outside niche.
- `CRAFT / VOICE / STYLE`: task-specific execution references.

For each admitted entry provide **an inspectable direct link**, date/context/evidence class, exact strength/limit and `ADOPT / ADAPT / REJECT / TEST` mechanism. An award page plus its original artifact can be linked separately. State clearly what is evidence of **craft**, **outcome**, or merely **inspiration**.

Keep a brief `REJECTED / WHY` note for deceptive near misses (e.g. beautiful but nonfunctional, popular but not original, uncited results, wrong-scale competitor), and 1–2 important **counterexamples** when they prevent blind imitation. Do not copy protected distinctive surface expression.

**Stop rule**: stop when new searches produce no new credible candidates/mechanisms or materially change no ranking, after domain coverage and obvious biases are checked. If source access is closed, stop claiming more and report gap rather than infinite research or speculative filling.

## 6. Evaluation and feedback without bureaucratic qualification

Prior to first use, quick practical checks across **at least two unlike domains**:
1. Top mobile site visual reference: award-winning desktop splash that fails a realistic mobile task ⇒ do not claim usability/commercial superiority.
2. Top social ad: popular/long-running ad with no audited conversion data ⇒ admit as inspected creative mechanism only, not proven sales performance.
3. Top technical/operational example: slick product landing without access to workflows/tests ⇒ do not infer robust engineering competence.
4. Small unknown practitioner with multiple inspected strong examples and corroboration ⇒ do not exclude solely for lack of fame.
5. Multiple search pages repeating the same award ⇒ count one source origin; vary the discovery channel.
6. Missing actual artifact / paywall / video inaccessible ⇒ exclude elite claim or return bounded PARTIAL.

These are **scenario checks**, not sealed qualification. Observe outputs in real research tasks, repair failure at source discovery, artifact inspection, expert criteria, evidence inference or downstream adaptation layer, and record targeted regressions. Full formal qualification is opt-in under `practical-use-and-optional-qualification.md`.

## Evidence grounding for this procedure

- [Webby judging criteria](https://www.webbyawards.com/judging-criteria/) are dimension-specific and explicitly include actual functionality; an award can be useful craft evidence, not a measured conversion guarantee.
- [D&AD 2026 judging model](https://www.dandad.org/awards/judging) uses practitioners; the judging sample and category scope still matter.
- [Vimeo curator FAQ](https://help.vimeo.com/hc/en-us/articles/12426104864017-FAQ-Vimeo-Staff-Picks) and [Behance curation FAQ](https://help.behance.net/hc/en-us/articles/204483974-Guide-Featured-Projects) establish curated discovery pathways.
- [TikTok official Top Ads dashboard help](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-use-the-top-ads-dashboard) documents available metrics and filtering; [TikTok authorization caveat](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/topads/mobile/en) makes sample-coverage limits explicit.
- [Nielsen Norman Group aesthetic-usability effect](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/aesthetic-usability-effect/) documents that good appearance can mask usability problems.
- These primary sources support the **principles and example channels**, not a claim that this new discovery procedure is empirically proved to outperform alternatives. Evaluate that claim with downstream comparisons and feedback.

## Expert gap/red team

- **Senior practitioner:** "Where is the real deployed artifact, not the teaser? What does it do well for its users, under what budget/context, and where is it weak?" -> direct inspection and counterexample mandatory.
- **Educator/assessor:** "Are you measuring the stated competence, or proxies like prizes, views or aesthetics?" -> independent evidence labels and field-specific claim definition.
- **Hiring manager:** "Would this reference actually improve a working deliverable and survive operational constraints?" -> transfer boundary, practical verification, downstream feedback.
- **Researcher:** "What share of exceptional work never entered the award gallery, and how many 'independent' lists share one source?" -> multi-channel exploration, provenance graph, discoverability bias.
