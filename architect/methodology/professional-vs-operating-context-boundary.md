# Professional Model vs Operating Context Boundary

## Purpose

Prevent reusable professional models from absorbing project-specific market, platform, account, organization, or experiment facts.

## Rule

A Professional Core or durable domain specialization contains transferable competencies, procedures, judgment, decision policies, failure modes, escalation rules and tool-use principles.

The following normally belong **outside** the reusable professional model:
- country-specific regulation or market conditions;
- current platform mechanics, UI, product availability, pricing or policy;
- a specific company's economics, authority, staffing, CRM, capacity or inventory state;
- a named account's configuration or performance history;
- a specific product, vehicle, campaign or experiment instance.

These belong in the consuming project's **live context / organization context / experiment brief** unless evidence shows the underlying principle is broadly transferable.

## Promotion test

Before promoting a project finding into a Professional Core or durable specialization, ask:
1. Would a strong practitioner need this principle across materially different countries, companies and platforms?
2. Does it remain valid when current UI, policy, account state and business facts change?
3. Is it professional judgment rather than a fact about one operating environment?
4. Is there evidence beyond one project instance?

If not, keep it in project context.

## Example

Reusable:
- do not optimize to cheap proxy conversions when downstream business quality degrades;
- verify measurement integrity before scaling;
- check marginal economics, delegated authority and operational capacity before material scale.

Project/live context:
- current UAE telemarketing rules;
- today's Meta campaign mechanics;
- current WhatsApp business messaging rules;
- Showroom 171 budget, inventory, staffing and CRM maturity.

Experiment instance:
- Toyota Yaris launch/test parameters.

A vehicle-specific experiment must not become a vehicle-specific specialist.
