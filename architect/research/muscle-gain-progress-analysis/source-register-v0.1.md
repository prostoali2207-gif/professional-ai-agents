## Evidence decisions

1. **Daily body mass:** no source supports tissue inference from a single reading. The skill treats isolated mass as a noisy observation and requires longitudinal aggregation for trend claims.
2. **Performance ≠ hypertrophy proof:** S01 and S10 support keeping strength/function and muscle-size outcomes distinct.
3. **RIR/RPE:** S04-S06 support contextual use with uncertainty; exact personal accuracy is not assumed.
4. **Body composition:** S07 directly supports hydration/glycogen confounding. Method-specific error thresholds must be obtained from applicable validation/reliability evidence rather than invented.
5. **Rate of gain:** S09 is retained only as contextual bodybuilding practice; S08 caps the claim because a universal optimum is not validated.
6. **Monitoring:** S02-S03 support multi-measure monitoring but do not justify a universal composite score.

## Freshness

Stable/slow: measurement principles, RIR uncertainty, causal/longitudinal discipline.
Review trigger: materially new position stand, systematic review that changes a critical claim, new device-specific measurement evidence used for a decision, or repeated practitioner correction.
Live retrieval required for device-specific reliability/MDC/TEM or current consensus not covered here.
