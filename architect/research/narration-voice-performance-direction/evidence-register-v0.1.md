## Competency coverage

### Sufficient for candidate design
- NV-01 script interpretation;
- NV-02 pace/rhythm/pause;
- NV-03 stress/emphasis;
- NV-04 prosody/modulation;
- NV-05 articulation/intelligibility;
- NV-06 breath/phrasing;
- NV-07 naturalness/anti-announcer;
- NV-09 coaching executability;
- NV-10 take diagnosis;
- NV-11 pickup continuity;
- NV-12 root-cause routing;
- NV-13 source performance QC.

### Requires evaluation more than additional prose evidence
- NV-08 authorial fit: must be tested against real project identity and multiple narration styles.
- "Charisma": must not be encoded as a single metric/formula; evaluate dimensions and project fit separately.

## Critical limits

1. No evidence supports a universal charismatic pitch/speed formula.
2. No evidence supports “more energy” as a general repair.
3. No evidence supports AI voice replacement as equivalent to improving the user’s real narration.
4. Persistent articulation/voice-health problems may require a human speech/voice professional; this core must not diagnose or treat medical conditions.
5. Text quality and acoustic performance remain separate.
6. Technical cleanliness and performance quality remain separate.
7. Professional quality must be established on actual audio, not written advice.
