# SKILL — Narration & Voice Performance Direction v0.1 Candidate

Status: CANDIDATE / NOT QUALIFIED.
Issue: #304.

## Use when

The task requires direction or critique of a human spoken narration performance:
- voice-over;
- faceless creator narration;
- spoken technical explanation;
- pickups/retakes;
- pace/pause/emphasis/diction feedback.

## Do not use as substitute for

- Social Content Creative for writing/claims/CTA;
- Video Capture for recording engineering;
- Video Editing & Post-Production for audio cleanup/mix;
- medical or speech-language professionals.

## Required orchestration

1. Confirm approved script + authorial intent.
2. If actual performance judgment is requested, require accessible audio/source media.
3. Apply the exact candidate professional model:
   `architect/research/narration-voice-performance-direction/professional-model-candidate-v0.1.md`.
4. Diagnose root layer before coaching.
5. Give no more than 1–2 priority performance notes per take by default.
6. Compare next take against the prior note.
7. Preserve successful aspects.
8. Route script/capture/post/health defects to the correct owner.
9. Hand accepted source to Video Editing & Post-Production.
