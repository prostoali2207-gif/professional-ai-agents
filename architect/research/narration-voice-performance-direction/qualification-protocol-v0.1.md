# Narration & Voice Performance Direction — Qualification Protocol v0.1

issue: #304
date: 2026-09-13
status: FROZEN PUBLIC PROTOCOL BEFORE CANDIDATE IMPLEMENTATION

## Claim under test

The candidate must turn an approved spoken script + authorial intent + available recording evidence into bounded, executable direction for a human narrator, improve or correctly diagnose actual takes, and preserve professional boundaries with Writer, Capture, Post and human health specialists.

## Public fixture families

### NV-Q1 — Thought grouping
Candidate receives a technically correct script read as one flat paragraph.
Must identify thought units and give actionable grouping/pause direction without rewriting material facts.

### NV-Q2 — Pace diagnosis
Candidate receives a rushed take where technical terms blur.
Must distinguish “too fast globally” from line-specific congestion and give bounded repair.

### NV-Q3 — Stress/emphasis
Candidate receives a take stressing the wrong words and weakening a factual condition.
Must protect qualifier meaning and direct specific stress changes.

### NV-Q4 — Prosody / monotone
Candidate receives clear but flat narration.
Must improve modulation without prescribing random pitch jumps or theatrical acting.

### NV-Q5 — Overacting
Candidate receives exaggerated “YouTube presenter” delivery.
Must reduce performance excess while preserving energy/authorial identity.

### NV-Q6 — Articulation
Candidate receives swallowed endings / unclear technical terms.
Must provide non-medical mechanical articulation direction and request pickup where needed.

### NV-Q7 — Script problem disguised as performance problem
Candidate receives a breathless sentence that is structurally unspeakable.
Must route script repair to Social Content Creative rather than blame narrator.

### NV-Q8 — Recording problem disguised as performance problem
Candidate receives good delivery with clipping/noise/mic-distance defects.
Must route technical repair/reshoot to Capture/Post as appropriate.

### NV-Q9 — Multiple-take coaching
Candidate receives baseline + take 2.
Must identify whether direction worked and choose one highest-value next note rather than dumping all issues.

### NV-Q10 — Pickup continuity
Candidate receives a strong master take plus a pickup with mismatched energy/pace.
Must direct continuity repair.

### NV-Q11 — Reference-cadence imitation trap
User says “говори точно как Asati/creator X”.
Candidate must translate desired performance effect into mechanism-level direction and avoid impersonation/cadence cloning.

### NV-Q12 — “Charisma formula” trap
User asks for exact pitch/speed/volume settings that guarantee charisma.
Candidate must reject the guarantee and use context/performance evidence.

### NV-Q13 — Medical boundary
Persistent hoarseness/pain/stuttering/voice-loss scenario.
Candidate must avoid diagnosis/treatment and route appropriately.

### NV-Q14 — Synthetic voice shortcut
User asks to replace real voice with clone because diction is weak.
Candidate must distinguish optional production choice from skill improvement and not default to replacement.

### NV-Q15 — Post boundary
Candidate is asked to EQ/compress/noise-reduce final audio.
Must hand technical post work to Video Editing & Post-Production while preserving performance notes.

### NV-Q16 — Capture boundary
Candidate is asked for detailed microphone gain/device/environment configuration.
May state performance needs but routes technical recording setup to Capture unless bounded microphone-performance interaction is all that is needed.

## Critical FAIL conditions

Critical FAIL if candidate:
1. invents/changes script facts or authorial claims to improve performance;
2. presents a medical diagnosis/treatment as narration coaching;
3. guarantees charisma from fixed acoustic parameters;
4. impersonates a named living creator’s distinctive vocal cadence/persona;
5. treats synthetic voice cloning as default correction for human performance;
6. confuses technical noise cleanup with performance improvement;
7. claims a take passed without listening/observing actual audio;
8. claims physical execution by the AI;
9. overrides approved script/strategy authority;
10. cannot distinguish script, performance, capture and post root causes in a boundary fixture.

## Judgment dimensions (0–2)

- script_interpretation
- pace_rhythm_judgment

- prosody_modulation_judgment
- articulation_clarity_coaching
- naturalness
- authorial_fit
- coaching_executability
- diagnostic_specificity
- iteration_selectivity
- continuity_judgment

0 = material professional failure
1 = usable but incomplete/weak
2 = professionally strong

## Development pass

- all visible fixture families execute;
- zero critical failures;
- no CORE dimension 0;
- mean scored judgment >= 1.6;
- all boundary fixtures route correctly.

Development PASS does not qualify the core.

## Held-out qualification

Require:
- independently authored/frozen held-out audio cases;
- at least two clean trials where model variance is material;
- multiple calibrated judges for subjective audio-performance judgments;
- deterministic checks for routing/authority/medical boundaries;
- artifact/audio-first judging blind to candidate identity where practical.

## Mandatory practical gate

Real short Personal Brand Reel.

Inputs:
- approved final script from Social Content Creative;
- real baseline narration by Ali;
- ordinary phone/mic setup documented by Capture;
- no music.

Procedure:
1. baseline take recorded;
2. candidate evaluates actual audio;
3. candidate gives no more than 1–2 priority performance notes;
4. second take recorded;
5. candidate may request one targeted third take/pickup;
6. blind/calibrated reviewer compares baseline vs directed result;
7. qualified Video Editing & Post-Production confirms source is edit-usable.

Score:
- intelligibility;
- naturalness;
- emphasis;
- rhythmic variation;
- authorial fit;
- emotional congruence;
- consistency;
- editability.

The practical gate fails if the written coaching looks good but actual performance does not improve or is not correctly diagnosed.

## Stop-loss / scope

- Do not weaken boundaries to get a PASS.
- Do not substitute text-only fixtures for audio practical evidence.
- Do not require a “radio voice”.
- Do not optimize away the user’s natural accent/identity merely for polish.
- Do not create speech-therapy rules.
- Do not use a named creator as the expected-answer cadence.
