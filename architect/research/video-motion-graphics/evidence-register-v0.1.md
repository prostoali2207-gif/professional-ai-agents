## Evidence interpretation

The sources jointly support an overlap-plus-boundary model:
- motion graphics can be integrated into editorial/post-production;
- substantial motion-design craft has distinct professional competencies and tooling;
- therefore a bounded EXTEND is plausible, but BUILD NEW remains a future architecture option if practical evaluation reveals separate ownership is necessary.

No source proves the candidate itself is competent. Qualification requires direct semantic and real-media practical evidence.
