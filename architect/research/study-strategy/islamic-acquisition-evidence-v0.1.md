# Islamic knowledge acquisition evidence v0.1

Status: targeted corrective research
Date: 2026-10-28
Incident: study-strategy underweighted exact memorization of matn/dalil in a beginner *Three Fundamental Principles* lesson.

## Root cause

The original candidate correctly protected:
- source fidelity;
- adaptive review load;
- non-universal memorization;
- delayed retrieval evidence.

But its Islamic specialization was primarily **negative/fidelity-oriented**:
- do not fabricate quotes;
- do not invent religious obligation;
- do not flatten authority layers.

It lacked a sufficiently explicit **positive model of traditional Islamic knowledge acquisition**. Consequently the generic memory-cost gate could dominate and produce the wrong practical recommendation: understand/recall the lesson while failing to proactively consider hifz of the curriculum's concise matn and core embedded proofs.

This is an architecture defect, not a one-off wording defect.

## Evidence convergence

### Ibn Jama'ah — *Tadhkirat al-Sami' wa al-Mutakallim*
In the learner's curriculum section, after prioritizing Qur'an, Ibn Jama'ah instructs the learner to memorize a concise text in each field and then study the explanation of those memorized texts with teachers.

Operational extraction:
`Qur'an priority -> concise matn hifz -> sharh with teacher -> progression`.

### Ibn 'Abd al-Barr — *Jami' Bayan al-'Ilm wa Fadlihi*
In the chapter on ranks of seeking knowledge, he states that طلب العلم has stages/ranks that should not be skipped and places memorization and understanding of the Qur'an at the beginning.

Operational extraction:
`tadarruj + hifz + fahm`, not either hifz-alone or fahm-alone.

### Bakr Abu Zayd — *Hilyat Talib al-'Ilm*
The section on how and in what ranks to seek knowledge emphasizes foundations, gradual progression, concise texts, mastering the foundation with a reliable teacher, and avoiding premature expansion.

Operational extraction:
`ta'sil -> concise foundation -> dabt with teacher -> tarqiyah`.

### Ibn 'Uthaymin — official foundation site
Multiple answers converge:
- beginner should be keen to memorize mutun;
- one starts with smaller knowledge before larger, memorizing a small work and building on it;
- Qur'an should be approached with memorization, understanding/reflection and action;
- retained knowledge is maintained by continued review/ta'ahud, mudhakarah and application.

Operational extraction:
`hifz + fahm + teacher-guided tadarruj + muraja'ah + 'amal`.

### Ibn Baz — official *Three Fundamental Principles* lessons
He reports that Muhammad ibn 'Abd al-Wahhab taught these foundations to students and the public for study and memorization so they would settle in the heart.

Operational extraction:
For this exact curriculum, matn memorization is not speculative; it is historically congruent with how the work was taught.

## Corrective design

The Islamic specialization must distinguish:
1. **matn** — positive exact-memorization candidate when verified and appropriately sized;
2. **embedded dalil** — exact/evidence-chain candidate by importance and curriculum role;
3. **sharh** — primarily understanding, recall of key distinctions, application;
4. **examples/fawa'id** — selective recall/reference;
5. **learner explanation** — understanding evidence, never source wording.

Exact memorization remains gated by verified wording/edition.

## Arabic policy

When learner capability permits, Arabic is preferred for exact memorization of Arabic matn and textual proofs because it preserves source identity. Translation supports understanding rather than replacing the exact target.

This is especially useful when the learner is simultaneously developing Arabic, provided fidelity is not sacrificed.

## Regression obligation

Every future study-strategy release must include:
- a matn-based beginner fixture;
- a matn + embedded dalil + sharh layering fixture;
- an anti-extreme fixture preventing memorization of the entire lecture.

## Qualification boundary

This correction strengthens domain fit but does not establish T1. Independent held-out evaluation is still required.
