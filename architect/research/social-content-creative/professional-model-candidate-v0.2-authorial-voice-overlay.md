## Purpose

Add a reusable capability for distinctive authorial spoken scripts without turning authorial voice into imitation, random slang, profanity, joke quotas, strategy drift or invented biography.

The target remains exact public-facing social copy/creative execution inside an approved brief.

## Added input — authorial context packet

When authorial voice is material, consume a bounded packet containing as available:
- approved brand/identity context;
- author relationship to audience;
- language(s) and natural register range;
- examples of the user's actual phrasing when legitimately supplied;
- preferred/forbidden tone boundaries;
- reference artifacts, explicitly marked as references rather than templates;
- series continuity state, if prior approved content exists.

Do not invent biography, personal experience, cultural identity, beliefs, slang habits, profanity habits or backstory to make the copy feel "authentic".

If project voice is insufficiently specified, create bounded alternatives inside the approved creative space. Do not silently manufacture a permanent persona contract.

## AV-01 — authorial voice / persona construction

Treat authorial voice as a system of decisions, not a bag of catchphrases.

Model at minimum when material:
- stance toward the subject;
- relationship to the viewer;
- lexical/register range;
- directness and certainty;
- allowed self-deprecation;
- recurring rhetorical devices;
- degree of technical/formal vs colloquial contrast;
- anti-patterns / cliché language;
- continuity with prior approved scripts.

A strong voice should remain recognizable after topic-specific nouns and catchphrases are removed.

Do not equate distinctiveness with:
- slang density;
- profanity;
- sentence fragments everywhere;
- fake vulnerability;
- invented personal anecdotes;
- random metaphors;
- excessive quirkiness.

## AV-02 — write for saying, not only reading

When the deliverable is spoken narration, test the wording as speech.

Prefer:
- speakable clause lengths;
- deliberate variation in sentence length;
- emphasis-ready word order;
- natural direct address when appropriate;
- controlled repetition;
- punctuation/structure that creates usable pause opportunities;
- technical terms placed where pronunciation and comprehension remain manageable.

Repair:
- report-like transitions;
- stacked subordinate clauses;
- visually neat but breathless sentences;
- repeated identical cadence;
- synthetic "creator" fillers;
- wording that only sounds conversational on page.

Do not claim actual vocal performance quality from text alone.

## AV-03 — humor mechanism judgment

Humor is optional creative space, not a quota.

Before adding a comic beat, identify its function:
- attention reset;
- tension/release;
- explanation by contrast;
- personality signal;
- self-deprecation;
- transition;
- callback/payoff;
- emphasis.

Candidate mechanisms may include:
- incongruity/surprise;
- contrast;
- misdirection;
- understatement;
- self-deprecation;
- callback;
- escalation;
- rule-of-three;
- register collision.

Choose no humor when the beat requires precision, gravity, trust, proof visibility or speed more than comic expression.

Never optimize for "jokes per minute".

## AV-04 — protect information and proof from humor

A joke may not:
- make a verified fact ambiguous;
- strengthen/weaken certainty beyond evidence;
- obscure an instruction the viewer must execute;
- trivialize a material limitation/disclosure;
- imply a personal result/experience that is not verified;
- replace the payoff the brief actually owes.

When humor and proof conflict, preserve proof and move/rewrite/remove the humor.

## AV-05 — continuity without catchphrase spam

Across a content series:
- preserve stable stance/relationship/register patterns where approved;
- vary surface wording and joke mechanisms;
- avoid repeating the same opener, CTA joke, transition or signature phrase by default;
- distinguish deliberate callbacks from accidental repetition;
- allow topic seriousness to modulate the voice without causing identity collapse.

A recognizable voice is not identical wording.

## AV-06 — diagnose generic AI voice causally

Do not fix genericness by merely adding slang.

Diagnose specific causes such as:
- empty intensifiers;
- generic suspense;
- over-balanced "not X, but Y" constructions;
- predictable three-beat motivational cadence;
- fake rhetorical questions;
- unnecessary "let's";
- abstract praise words;
- mechanically tidy transitions;
- repeated conclusion formulas;
- pseudo-personal language unsupported by real context.

Repair the responsible layer while preserving facts and structure.

## AV-07 — reference abstraction / anti-imitation

For every strong reference:
1. identify the communication problem it solves;
2. identify transferable mechanisms;
3. identify context-specific/non-transferable expression;
4. generate a materially different execution for this project.

Never copy or closely paraphrase:
- distinctive catchphrases;
- signature greetings/sign-offs;
- memorable joke wording;
- creator-specific verbal identity;
- distinctive sequences of phrasing.

If the user asks for "exactly like creator X", translate the request into mechanism-level attributes and produce a project-owned alternative.

## AV-08 — controlled profanity is optional

Profanity is neither authenticity nor charisma by default.

Use it only when all are true:
- project/user context permits it;
- it fits the actual approved voice rather than a fabricated persona;
- it earns a clear function such as emphasis, incongruity or emotional truth;
- the same beat is weaker without it after comparison;
- audience/platform/brand/reputation constraints do not outweigh the benefit.

Do not sprinkle profanity as filler or imitate another creator's profanity profile.

## Authorial divergence rule

When voice direction is open, create genuinely different authorial mechanisms before converging. Differences may include:
- dry/understated vs animated;
- technical-deadpan vs conversational;
- self-deprecating vs assertive;
- sparse humor vs humor-forward;
- compact punchy vs story-led.

Do not create three variants that differ only by synonyms/slang.

Selection must be based on:
- fit to approved audience/context;
- clarity;
- truth preservation;
- speakability;
- distinctiveness;
- continuity;
- production feasibility;
- derivative risk.

## Critique rule

When an authorial script feels weak:
1. restate communication job and voice constraints;
2. locate the actual weak lines/mechanisms;
3. classify failure: generic wording / persona drift / forced humor / poor speakability / proof conflict / derivative reference / overperformance;
4. repair only the responsible layer;
5. re-read the relevant passage as spoken language.

Do not answer "make it punchier" with indiscriminate shortening, slang or profanity.

## Handoff addition

When authorial voice is material, downstream handoff should include:
- exact approved script;
- authorial intent notes only where performance materially depends on them;
- pronunciation/technical-term flags where necessary;
- optional pause/emphasis cues as guidance, not proof of actual delivery;
- reference-mechanism notes when relevant;
- unresolved performance dependencies.

Do not over-direct every word. Physical vocal-performance coaching remains outside this candidate's qualification claim.

## Boundary — performed narration

This overlay may optimize text for performance and provide bounded read intent.

It does not establish professional competence in:
- vocal prosody execution;
- diction/articulation coaching;
- voice acting;
- vocal health;
- microphone performance;
- actual take direction;
- acoustic recording engineering.

Route those needs separately unless/until a compatible qualified capability exists.
