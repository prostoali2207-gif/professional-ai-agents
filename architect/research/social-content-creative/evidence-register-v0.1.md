## Evidence architecture decisions

### Stable core candidates
The following are professional judgment invariants, subject to further triangulated craft evidence:
- BR-01 brief fidelity and explicit ambiguity handling.
- CR-01 truthful hook/payoff alignment.
- CP-01 clarity/specificity and avoidance of unsupported intensifiers.
- CJ-01 genuine divergence versus cosmetic paraphrase.
- CJ-02 selection based on brief/evidence rather than trend/taste alone.
- TR-01/02 claim grounding and implication-level truthfulness.
- PD-02 production feasibility from declared resources.
- EX-01 controlled-variable preservation.
- AU-01 distinction between creation and release authority.

### LIVE bindings
- Current Instagram/Meta placement dimensions, safe zones, feature availability, recommendation mechanics, music/audio restrictions and ad/organic surface capabilities.
- Current YouTube/Shorts capabilities, packaging surfaces and policy.
- Current Telegram surface capabilities.
- UAE laws/regulations and any local licensing/advertising requirements.
- Business facts: price, availability, mileage, unit-specific equipment, condition/repair history, warranty, finance, discount, stock status and permissions.

## Gaps still requiring evidence before candidate implementation
1. BR/CR/CP/CJ: triangulated copywriting, creative development, brief interpretation and critique evidence from strong professional/educational sources plus authentic comparative artifacts.
2. PD-01/PD-02: production-direction and visual-storytelling evidence suitable for small-team social/video production.
3. AC-01: accessibility/readability evidence for mobile social video/text overlays without freezing volatile platform UI geometry.
4. IP-01: copyright/reference-transformation professional guidance suitable for creative-agent boundaries; legal interpretation remains escalated.
5. PL-01: refreshed official Meta/Instagram and YouTube primary documentation before qualification run.
