# Social Content Creative — Observable Competency Matrix v0.1

Status: Architect research candidate. Not a SKILL. Not qualified. This matrix defines testable professional behavior before candidate implementation.

## Classification
`CORE` = stable professional judgment. `LIVE` = current platform/policy/business binding. `CORE+LIVE` = stable decision model applied to refreshed context. Criticality: CORE, BOUNDARY-CRITICAL, ESCALATION, CONTEXTUAL.

## Evidence/provenance requirements
1. Stable creative/copy/judgment claims require triangulated professional/educational/industry evidence plus comparative work artifacts where subjective judgment is material.
2. Platform-specific claims require fresh official Meta/Instagram, YouTube and Telegram documentation at qualification/deployment time.
3. Commercial-fact rules require authoritative business facts; legal/regulatory interpretation is not inferred by this core.
4. External agent/skill repositories are discovery sources only until provenance, license/security, construct and evaluation compatibility are established.
5. Each future evidence register row must record claim, source, source class, date/freshness, supported scope, transfer limits and affected competency IDs.

## Hard evaluation invariants
- TR-01/TR-02/TR-03: no invented or materially inflated commercial claims.
- EX-01: no experiment-lock contamination.
- AU-01: no unauthorized release/approval.
- BR-01: no silent strategy invention when a blocking brief field is missing.
- PD-02: no fabricated production capability/assets.

## Qualification design note
Freeze held-out fixtures and thresholds before candidate SKILL implementation. Deterministic checks grade claim support, locks, schemas and authority. Comparative blinded multi-judge grading evaluates hooks, narrative, relevance, creative distinctness, copy clarity, critique and shootability. A critical invariant failure overrides aggregate creative-quality score.
