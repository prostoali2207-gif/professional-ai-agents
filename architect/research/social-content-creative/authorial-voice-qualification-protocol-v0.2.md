## Why protocol revision is required

Applied Personal Brand work exposed a material failure not explicitly discriminated by v0.1:

A candidate can:
- preserve facts;
- sound conversational;
- avoid generic AI phrases;
- use a benchmark correctly in rationale;

and still produce **safe pedagogic collapse** — polished consultant/teacher language instead of creator-practitioner execution.

Observed applied failure:
- elite niche benchmark existed;
- candidate rationale correctly described artifact-led judgment;
- final script still used teacherly constructions such as:
  - "Я бы не оставлял..."
  - "Поэтому..."
  - "Вот такие вещи я и смотрю..."

This protocol adds explicit evidence requirements for benchmark-to-execution transfer, creator-native execution, verbal proof economy and closure behavior.

## New public fixture families

### AV-Q13 — Safe pedagogic collapse

Input:
- creator-led commercial social brief;
- verified artifact;
- non-technical buyer;
- strong benchmark packet;
- baseline candidate that is correct, clear, conversational and teacherly.

Expected:
- diagnose teacher/consultant mode as the wrong execution mode for this brief;
- produce creator-practitioner alternatives;
- make judgment/action observable earlier;
- remove unnecessary lesson recap;
- preserve truth and buyer comprehension.

Critical fail:
- only sharpens wording;
- only adds slang;
- output remains a polished mini-lesson.

### AV-Q14 — Benchmark without execution transfer

Input:
- benchmark packet with clear functional mechanisms;
- candidate rationale that accurately names them;
- draft whose execution remains pre-benchmark generic.

Expected:
- identify mismatch between rationale and artifact;
- show exactly what the benchmark changes in opening/proof timing/explanation/closure;
- materially rebuild the execution;
- retain anti-imitation.

Critical fail:
- cites benchmark as justification while final artifact does not visibly change;
- copies reference expression.

### AV-Q15 — Visual proof economy

Input:
- short-form screen-recorded artifact where several facts are directly visible.

Expected:
- use speech for tension, judgment, non-obvious context;
- allow visuals to prove obvious fields/interactions;
- avoid narrating every visible UI step.

Critical fail:
- voiceover redundantly reads the interface;
- removes needed context so far that viewer cannot understand the proof.

### AV-Q16 — Closure without lecture recap

Input:
- payoff has already been shown visually and semantically.

Expected:
- stop cleanly, use short stance/reaction, or allow visual close;
- do not add generic summary merely for completeness.

Critical fail:
- ending repeats the already-proven lesson in generic terms;
- closure becomes generic agency positioning.

### AV-Q17 — Fake spontaneity trap

Input:
- user asks to make a polished script "more real" with fillers, profanity, fake stumbles or invented off-the-cuff reactions.

Expected:
- reject fake spontaneity as a shortcut;
- create natural live-practitioner language from actual artifact/decision;
- do not invent anecdote/emotion/reaction.

## New judgment dimensions (0–2)

Add:
- creator_native_execution
- earned_stance_quality
- benchmark_transfer_visibility
- visual_proof_economy
- closure_selectivity

0 = material failure
1 = usable but weak
2 = professionally strong

Lock:
- audience;
- service scope;
- verified facts;
- Content Architecture;
- benchmark packet;
- authorial context.

Compare:
A. qualified parent baseline;
B. prior v0.2-style safe/pedagogic candidate;
C. v0.4 candidate.

Required:
- C must be materially more creator-native than A and B;
- C must preserve truth and strategy;
- benchmark effect must be visible in the artifact, not only rationale;
- C must not become fake-high-energy, slang-heavy, derivative or less comprehensible;
- human project owner acceptance remains project-fit evidence only.
