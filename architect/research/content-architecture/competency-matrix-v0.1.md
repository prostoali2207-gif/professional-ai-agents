# Content Architecture — competency matrix v0.1

Status: Architect research artifact. Candidate competency model; not qualified.

## Scope classes

- CORE: owned professional judgment.
- BOUNDARY-CRITICAL: must be understood well enough to preserve another owner's contract.
- ESCALATION: detect and route; do not perform specialist decisioning.
- CONTEXTUAL: live/domain/project knowledge loaded when relevant.

## CA-01 Brief and lock interpretation — CORE

**Situation:** approved strategist brief or content assignment arrives.

**Observable capability:** reduces it to audience/relevance signal, intended effect, approved mechanism, desired action, tested variable, hard locks, bounded choices, and open creative space without silently changing them.

**Expert discriminator:** distinguishes genuine structural freedom from a strategy change.

**Failure modes:** scope theft; treating an assumption as a lock; missing a controlled variable; compensating for a weak brief by inventing strategy.

**Evaluation:** conflicting or incomplete briefs; identify what can proceed, what is blocked, and exact owner for escalation.

## CA-02 Attention-contract design — CORE

**Situation:** material needs an opening/hook.

**Observable capability:** specifies what the viewer should perceive/understand in the opening, the curiosity/tension/relevance created, and the payoff obligation, without requiring clickbait or final wording.

**Judgment:** opening strength depends on relevance, specificity, evidence, mechanism and medium; no universal '3-second rule' is encoded as stable doctrine.

**Failure modes:** generic shock; unsupported price/result hook; disconnected hook-payoff; logos/greetings before relevance by habit; identical hook family for every brief.

**Evaluation:** choose among plausible openings and explain trade-offs; reject a high-attention but misleading opening.

## CA-03 Narrative / information architecture — CORE

**Situation:** decide block sequence.

**Observable capability:** creates the minimum sequence that lets the intended audience understand, believe and act; each block has a communication job and dependency.

**Cues:** prior knowledge, objection burden, causal dependency, reveal timing, proof availability, medium constraints.

**Failure modes:** fixed template use; redundant blocks; explanation before relevance; proof after viewer must already trust the claim; burying the payoff.

**Evaluation:** structurally reorder the same facts for different objectives without changing strategy.

## CA-04 Proof architecture and claim-evidence fit — CORE + BOUNDARY-CRITICAL

**Situation:** content contains persuasive or commercial claims.

**Observable capability:** maps each material claim/implication to sufficient supplied evidence, decides where evidence must become visible/stated, and blocks unsupported strengthening.

**Expert discriminator:** notices implied claims created by sequencing, cropping, labels or juxtaposition, not only explicit sentences.

**Failure modes:** brochure/model fact -> vehicle-specific claim; unsupported scarcity/warranty/price; proof too late; decorative testimonial unrelated to claim.

**Evaluation:** adversarial fact packets containing missing, stale, ambiguous and conflicting evidence.

## CA-05 Pacing and information-density planning — CORE

**Situation:** choose approximate duration and tempo zones.

**Observable capability:** allocates time according to comprehension, evidence visibility, emotional/cognitive transition and platform context; identifies removable material.

**Boundary:** broad timing ranges/semantic tempo belong here; exact cut points, retiming and edit rhythm execution belong to Video Post-Production.

**Failure modes:** fast = good; arbitrary duration target; proof flashed too quickly to verify; padding.

**Evaluation:** same mechanism with simple vs evidence-heavy content should receive different pacing rationale.

## CA-06 Offer and CTA placement — CORE + BOUNDARY-CRITICAL

**Situation:** approved offer/action exists.

**Observable capability:** decides when the offer/CTA becomes contextually earned and how preceding structure leads to it.

**Boundary:** does not choose a new offer, destination, funnel objective or final CTA wording when locked upstream.

**Failure modes:** CTA before trust/proof when the mechanism requires proof; competing actions; changing destination for convenience.

## CA-07 Visual communication requirements — CORE + BOUNDARY-CRITICAL

**Situation:** structure depends on visuals.

**Observable capability:** specifies what must be visible to communicate/prove each block, what information cannot be obscured, and whether available assets can support the requirement.

**Boundary:** does not prescribe unnecessary frame-level edit operations, grading, sound mix, transitions or render settings.

**Failure modes:** aesthetic shot list without communication job; impossible asset requirement; visual that contradicts text/proof.

## CA-08 Creative divergence and selection — CORE

**Situation:** meaningful open solution space remains.

**Observable capability:** generates structurally distinct options (different information order, attention mechanism, proof reveal, comparison logic, perspective) and selects against the brief rather than taste.

**Failure modes:** pseudo-divergence; first-idea convergence; copying a viral reference; novelty that damages function.

**Evaluation:** blind comparative rubric for fidelity, distinctness, proof logic and functional communication.

## CA-09 Platform-context adaptation — CONTEXTUAL

**Situation:** Instagram, YouTube, Telegram or another surface changes viewing/interaction constraints.

**Observable capability:** retrieves current authoritative platform guidance when materially relevant and adapts structure without converting a current recommendation into universal professional doctrine.

**Failure modes:** remembered stale specs; importing short-form rules into Telegram; changing strategic mechanism during adaptation.

**Knowledge routing:** LIVE_RESEARCH for volatile platform behavior/specifications; retained stable principle only when genuinely platform-independent.

## CA-10 Experiment-integrity preservation — BOUNDARY-CRITICAL

**Situation:** content is part of a registered experiment.

**Observable capability:** preserves tested and controlled variables through structural design and flags contamination.

**Boundary:** does not set KPI, estimand, threshold, sample, test window or SCALE/ITERATE/KILL.

**Failure modes:** variant changes hook + proof + duration; analytics recommendation smuggled into architecture; premature response to noisy performance.

## CA-11 Structural observability handoff — BOUNDARY-CRITICAL

**Situation:** downstream Analytics needs to compare planned vs executed content.

**Observable capability:** emits identifiers for planned hook family/job, semantic blocks, intended proof/offer/CTA positions, planned duration range and execution locks.

**Boundary correction:** this is metadata, not analytics instrumentation ownership. Analytics owns event schema, KPI logic, attribution, denominators, outcome maturity and experiment decisioning.

## CA-12 Creator freedom and handoff — CORE

**Situation:** architecture moves to Content Creator.

**Observable capability:** states `must preserve`, `bounded`, `may choose`, and `must escalate`; leaves final wording/performance/creative craft open where strategy does not require a lock.

**Failure modes:** writing the whole final script; over-specification that removes Creator judgment; under-specification that permits mechanism drift.

## CA-13 Revision and critique — CORE

**Situation:** feedback or asset constraints require change.

**Observable capability:** diagnoses whether the issue is structure, missing evidence, strategy, copy, asset, edit execution, preference or platform constraint; revises only the responsible layer.

**Failure modes:** patch accumulation; user-pressure capitulation; aesthetic preference overriding evidence; strategy redesign disguised as cleanup.

## CA-14 Commercial-truth discipline — BOUNDARY-CRITICAL

**Situation:** price, stock, mileage, condition, history, specs, warranty, finance, discount, result, testimonial or scarcity may be communicated.

**Observable capability:** uses only authoritative supplied/live business facts within their scope and freshness; marks unresolved facts rather than inferring.

**Knowledge routing:** project/business fact source or live authoritative source; never model prior as authority.

## CA-15 IP/reference independence — BOUNDARY-CRITICAL

**Situation:** competitor/reference creative is used.

**Observable capability:** abstracts mechanism/decision principles without copying protected/distinctive expression or treating trend popularity as evidence of suitability.

## Out-of-scope / escalation matrix

- Audience / strategic mechanism / funnel objective -> Strategist.
- Market/competitor evidence collection -> Market Intelligence.
- Final copy/script/caption/title/thumbnail wording -> Content Creator.
- Frame-level edit, audio, captions implementation, color, render -> Video Post-Production.
- KPI/attribution/causal interpretation/SCALE-KILL -> Analytics.
- Client conversation/qualification/closing -> Sales / Lead Conversion.
- Publish/release -> authorized human/publisher.

## Knowledge Packaging Audit

- EMBED_CORE: brief-lock reasoning, attention/payoff continuity, sequencing logic, proof architecture, pacing principles, creator-handoff boundary.
- PROCEDURAL_MODULE: architecture generation/revision procedure; claim-to-proof audit; experiment-lock audit.
- REFERENCE_MODULE: comparative examples of structural patterns with provenance and scope notes.
- LIVE_RESEARCH: platform behavior/specs/policies and current creative guidance.
- TOOL_BACKED: verified project facts, asset inventory/metadata, experiment brief/schema validation.
- ESCALATE: legal interpretation, unsupported commercial fact, unresolved strategic contradiction.
