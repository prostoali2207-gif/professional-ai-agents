# Content Architecture — profession reconstruction v0.1

Status: Architect research artifact. Not a SKILL. Not qualified.

## Target work

The target does not map cleanly to the generic title `Content Analyst`. The real work is a bounded creative-architecture function between strategy and execution:

`approved strategic brief -> communication/creative structure -> creator-ready architecture -> downstream production`

Working profession name: **Content Architecture & Creative Structure Practitioner**.

This is a composite professional boundary drawing from creative strategy, content strategy, direct-response creative briefing, narrative/information architecture, and pre-production editorial planning. It deliberately excludes ownership that belongs to campaign strategy, final copywriting, media buying, editing/post-production, measurement decisioning, publishing, or sales.

## Primary responsibility

Transform an approved content/experiment brief into a structurally coherent, evidence-grounded content architecture that specifies what each part of the material must accomplish, in what sequence, with what proof, approximate timing/pacing, visual communication requirements, and handoff constraints, while preserving strategy and commercial truth.

## Core outputs

1. attention contract / hook job, without necessarily writing final hook copy;
2. semantic block sequence and the job of each block;
3. promise -> explanation/demonstration -> proof/payoff -> action continuity;
4. proof architecture: which claim needs which evidence and where it must appear;
5. timing and pacing ranges derived from information density and platform/context constraints rather than fixed formulas;
6. offer/CTA placement and transition logic, while preserving strategist-approved objective/destination;
7. visual evidence requirements and production feasibility constraints;
8. experiment-lock preservation when the brief is an experiment;
9. explicit free/bounded/locked execution space for Content Creator;
10. structural metadata for downstream observation, without taking Analytics authority.

## What this profession does not own

- target audience selection;
- funnel role or campaign objective;
- strategic mechanism/hypothesis selection;
- primary KPI, threshold, decision horizon, SCALE/ITERATE/KILL;
- vehicle/offer/commercial fact creation;
- final script, caption, title, thumbnail copy, or full public-facing prose;
- detailed edit decisions, frame-level cut execution, audio, color, captions burn-in, render/export;
- publication or sales communication.

A practitioner may expose an upstream contradiction and propose bounded structural alternatives, but cannot silently redesign strategy.

## Difficult professional decisions

### D1 — What belongs in the opening?
Strong work identifies the minimum truthful information/event that makes the intended audience understand relevance quickly enough to continue, while avoiding clickbait whose promised payoff is not delivered.

### D2 — What information order produces comprehension and persuasion?
The practitioner decides whether proof should precede explanation, whether a comparison needs orientation first, whether an objection should be answered before an offer, and what can be omitted without breaking credibility or meaning.

### D3 — How much proof is necessary and where?
The practitioner distinguishes a claim from decoration, identifies evidence-bearing moments, and places proof where it resolves the viewer's likely uncertainty rather than appending generic social proof mechanically.

### D4 — What pace is appropriate?
Fast cutting is not a universal objective. Pace follows information density, comprehension burden, proof visibility, emotional/intellectual transition, platform behavior, and the approved mechanism.

### D5 — What is structure versus copy versus edit?
This is a key boundary judgment. The practitioner should specify the function of a line/beat/visual, not necessarily author the exact public-facing wording; specify timing zones and evidence requirements, not perform final timeline editing.

### D6 — When is an upstream brief structurally unworkable?
If the promised value cannot be truthfully supported, required proof/assets are missing, the CTA conflicts with the funnel objective, or experiment locks make the proposed structure impossible, the practitioner escalates instead of improvising strategy or facts.

## Expert-vs-average discriminator

A weak practitioner:
- applies a fixed Hook -> Problem -> Solution -> CTA template regardless of brief;
- optimizes for fast cuts rather than comprehension;
- treats a hook as clickbait text rather than an attention contract;
- adds proof late as decoration;
- writes the whole script and thereby collapses into Content Creator;
- changes audience/offer/CTA to make the structure easier;
- uses one viral reference as a universal rule;
- requests analytics instrumentation beyond what the content architecture needs.

A strong practitioner:
- reduces the brief to its communication mechanism and constraints;
- creates multiple structurally distinct options when meaningful creative space remains;
- selects order based on audience question, proof burden and intended action;
- maps every material claim to sufficient evidence;
- distinguishes structural timing from editor-owned frame-level timing;
- preserves experimental invariants and commercial truth;
- marks assumptions and missing evidence explicitly;
- creates a handoff that gives the Creator meaningful freedom without strategic drift.

## Evidence and source classes

### Stable professional/craft evidence
Use creative-strategy practice, content/information architecture, narrative construction, editorial planning, advertising/communication research, and comparative authentic artifacts. No single practitioner's template is universal doctrine.

### Current platform evidence
Platform-specific guidance is a live binding. Current TikTok guidance, for example, explicitly frames creative as a structured sequence of hook, body and close, with early value communication and a clear CTA. Google/YouTube's ABCD guidance separates Attention, Branding, Connection and Direction and is presented as data-backed creative guidance. These support the existence of communication-structure decisions, not a timeless mandatory formula.

### Internal evidence
Own experiments, retained creative-performance evidence, verified business facts, content renders, and downstream sales outcomes have priority for local decisioning when construct-valid and mature enough.

## Initial source set

- Google/Think with Google, `ABCDs of effective creative` — platform creative guidance based on collaboration with Ipsos and review by Nielsen/Kantar; supports distinct attention/connection/direction functions.

## Boundary judgment on the existing project hypothesis

The user's proposed boundary is substantially supported, with two corrections:

1. `Content Analyst` should not own Analytics instrumentation design. It may emit structural observability metadata (planned hook type, block identities, proof/offer/CTA positions, intended variable locks) so Analytics can compare plan vs execution, but KPI/event/attribution design remains Analytics/Strategist territory.
2. Visual requirements must stop at communication/evidence needs and production constraints. Frame-level editorial selection, cut timing, transitions, audio, captions implementation, color and render QC remain Video Post-Production.

## Architecture implication

A separate professional core is justified because this function contains repeatable judgment that is neither strategy nor copywriting nor editing. However, it should be a narrow core with strong handoff contracts, not a general 'creative strategist' super-role.
