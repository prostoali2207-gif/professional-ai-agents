# Exercise Technique & Selection — evidence register v0.1

Status: pre-SKILL evidence base
Search/review date: 2026-09-21

## Evidence policy

Material behavior is based on source-appropriate evidence. This register distinguishes authoritative synthesis, systematic reviews, primary trials/laboratory studies, and clinical boundary sources. Biomechanics findings are not converted into universal form rules unless evidence supports that scope.

## Derived stable principles

1. **Goal-relative technique:** technique quality must be evaluated against the intended task, target adaptation and constraints, not visual conformity alone. Supported by ETS-001, ETS-004, ETS-006, ETS-007.
2. **ROM is contextual:** full ROM has broad support for strength/lower-body hypertrophy, while long-length partials can be useful in some muscles; no universal ROM rule. ETS-001—003.
3. **Stability is a trade-off, not a virtue score:** machines and free weights can both support hypertrophy; stability changes loadability/skill/stabilizer demands and strength specificity. ETS-001, ETS-004.
4. **Resistance profile matters but does not mechanically determine hypertrophy:** it changes joint-angle loading and specificity; empirical outcomes must constrain inference. ETS-006.
5. **Anthropometry is a modifier, not destiny:** segment lengths can alter geometry and some performance relations, but available prediction evidence is limited/weak. ETS-009—011.
6. **Fatigue changes kinematics:** later-rep deviation can be an effort/load signal; it is not automatically pathology or incompetence. ETS-012—013.
7. **2D vision has a claim ceiling:** use visible qualitative/plane-specific evidence, state uncertainty, request better views, and never manufacture precise 3D joint loading from uncontrolled media. ETS-014—016.
8. **Pain terminates non-clinical diagnosis:** the skill may advise stopping/adjusting the exercise and seeking appropriate clinical assessment; it may not identify injured tissue or prescribe rehabilitation. ETS-017—018.\n9. **Cueing is an intervention to test, not a truth claim:** attentional-focus evidence supports task-relevant cue design, but response varies and long-term superiority is not guaranteed. ETS-019.

## Explicitly rejected overclaims

- There is one universally safe squat/deadlift/press form.
- Knees past toes, lumbar motion, stance width or bar path is automatically dangerous absent task-specific evidence.
- Long femurs require exercise X.
- Free weights are better for hypertrophy because they use more stabilizers.
- Machine stability is always better for hypertrophy.
- EMG proves which exercise grows a muscle most.
- A single photo proves a dynamic technique error.
- A 2D pose-estimation angle is a clinical joint-load measurement.
- Pain in location X means injury Y.
- Any rep slowing or changing near failure is bad form.
