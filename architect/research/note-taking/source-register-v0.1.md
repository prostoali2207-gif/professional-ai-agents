# Note-Taking — source register v0.1

Status: PRE-SKILL EVIDENCE
Date: 2026-09-23

## Reuse provenance

This source register inherits the note-taking evidence previously assembled for `lecture-note-taking-coach@0.1.0-candidate` and narrows it to the domain layer. Pedagogy-specific evidence is owned by `learning-coach`.

## Sources retained for note-taking decisions

### N1 — Flanigan et al. / systematic review and meta-analysis (2022)
"The effect of notetaking method on academic performance."
Contemporary Educational Psychology 68:102025.
DOI: 10.1016/j.cedpsych.2021.102025

Decision impact: avoid a universal handwriting-vs-typing rule; medium is secondary to processing, distraction, task requirements, and accessibility.

### N2 — Titsworth & Kiewra (2004)
"Spoken Organizational Lecture Cues and Student Notetaking as Facilitators of Student Learning."
Contemporary Educational Psychology 29(4):447-461.
DOI: 10.1016/j.cedpsych.2003.12.001

Decision impact: train recognition of structural lecture cues and convert them into note hierarchy/slots.

### N3 — Luo, Kiewra & Samuelson (2016)
"Revising Lecture Notes: How Revision, Pauses, and Partners Affect Note Taking and Achievement."
Instructional Science 44:45-67.

Decision impact: separate rough capture from targeted revision; first-pass notes need not be polished.

### N4 — Kiewra, Colliot & Lu (2018)
"Note This: How to Improve Student Note Taking."
IDEA Paper #73. ERIC ED588353.

Decision impact: explicit instruction and structured support can improve note completeness/utility; note-taking should be treated as a trainable skill.

### N5 — Karpicke & Blunt (2011)
"Retrieval Practice Produces More Learning than Elaborative Studying with Concept Mapping."
Science 331(6018):772-775.
DOI: 10.1126/science.1199327

Decision impact: notes should support active retrieval, not only passive rereading.

## Limits

- Evidence does not justify a universal compression ratio.
- Exact wording is task-dependent; source-critical items may require preservation/verification.
- Retrieval value is one dimension of note quality, not permission to omit necessary fidelity.
