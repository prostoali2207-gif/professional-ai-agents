# Knowledge and sources: Service Offer Packaging & Pricing v0.1

Collected: 2026-10-23 by live web research (fetched pages inspected, not search snippets).

## A. Practitioner methods (primary-source practitioners; doctrine, not controlled experiments)

Evidence grade: PRACTITIONER_DOCTRINE. Strong for method, weak for any claim that it raises revenue.

### Blair Enns (value pricing for creative/professional services)
Source: https://www.teamwork.com/blog/value-masterclass-key-takeaways/ (masterclass summary, secondary), https://archive.davidairey.com/dealing-with-designers/pricing-creativity-blair-enns/ (listed, not inspected)
- Value is set by the buyer's perception; speak to the specific buyer.
- Do not price by hours or headcount, otherwise efficiency (including AI) becomes a discount demand.
- Value conversation before proposing a solution: desired future state, how success is measured, what achieving it is worth, what the buyer would pay for it.
- Present three options that differ in ambition, risk and investment; the middle is usually the safest pick.
- Do not defend or discount when budgets tighten; change the scope instead. Do not hide AI use; explain where it adds speed and where judgment stays.

### Jonathan Stark (fixed-fee value pricing for freelancers)
Source: https://contentsnare.com/value-based-pricing-jonathan-stark/ (secondary summary)
- Discovery conversation: why it matters, milestones, why now, why you.
- Start from the normal hourly estimate and add a premium of at least 85% for surprises and delivered value.
- Offer three tiers; the higher tiers anchor expectations.
- State a fixed fee as a quote, not an estimate.

### Brian Casel (productized service)
Source: https://shop.smashingmagazine.com/2015/01/lessons-learned-building-a-productized-service/
- Do-it-for-you setup should be a paid, required part of the offer; the optional or DIY path produced more cancellations.
- Ongoing support is part of the offer.
- A paid discovery phase compensates scoping work.
- Do not automate before the bottlenecks are understood; the service, not the software, is what customers pay for.
- Limit: the article does not define a scope or price model explicitly; those parts are inference.

## B. Market price bands (orientation only)

Evidence grade: VENDOR_SELF_REPORT. All numbers come from agencies, studios or vendors selling these services, via SEO-style cost guides. Sample, method and recency of their prices are unknown. Use only as order-of-magnitude orientation, never as "the market price". Currency conversion is deliberately not done here (no verified FX); the agent must fetch current FX when comparing.

### Russian-speaking market, rubles (landing)
Source: https://granulastudio.ru/skolko-stoit-lending/
- Template landing (Tilda/Readymag): 10,000-30,000 RUB, 1-3 days.
- "Start" custom landing: from 45,000-50,000 RUB, about 2 weeks (5-7 sections, unique design, adaptive layout, request form, basic SEO).
- "Business" from 90,000 RUB, about 3 weeks; "Product" from 140,000 RUB, about 4 weeks.
- Modifiers: CRM/1C/payment integration +10,000-80,000; animation +15,000-40,000; copywriting 5,000-15,000; rush +20-50%; client-supplied texts and photos -10-25%.

### Russian-speaking market, rubles (CRM)
- Own CRM, one vendor: 80,000-140,000 RUB build, 5,000 RUB/month support, additions from 15,000. Includes DB design, API, web interface, roles, deployment, docs, training, source code. Source: https://aiorkestrator.ru/blog/skolko-stoit-crm-dlya-malogo-biznesa/
- amoCRM turnkey setup for small business: 50,000-150,000 RUB; integrations from 2,000-5,000 RUB each; custom widget from 15,000; maintenance 6,000-25,000 RUB/month. Source: https://cmdf5.ru/blog/tsena-amocrm-v-2026-godu-dlya-malogo-biznesa
- Dispersion warning: other studios' result titles (not inspected) state custom CRM "from 300,000", "from 700,000" and "from 1,000,000" RUB. The same service differs by roughly an order of magnitude by seller tier and scope. This spread is the main fact the agent must respect.

### UAE, AED (web)
Source: https://www.hikmahaiagency.com/blog/website-development-cost-dubai-2026
- Landing page: freelancer 1,500-4,000; boutique agency 4,000-10,000; premium 8,000-20,000.
- Business website 5-15 pages: freelancer 3,000-8,000 (WordPress); boutique 8,000-20,000; premium 20,000-50,000.
- Custom web app (agency, Next.js/React): simple 35,000-70,000; mid 70,000-150,000; complex 150,000-400,000+.
- CRM integration 2,000-10,000; Arabic/RTL 2,000-8,000. Freelancers about 30-60% below agencies.

### USD (international agencies)
- Custom CRM: starter one pipeline $3,500 fixed, 2 weeks; basic $15,000-40,000, 4-8 weeks; mid $40,000-90,000; advanced $90,000-150,000+. Maintenance 15-20% of build per year. Source: https://0arch.io/blog/cost-to-build-custom-crm
- Automation: single workflow $1,500-12,000; light retainer $1,000-3,500/month; full-service retainer $4,000-12,000/month; setup fee $2,000-12,000; messy-API integrations +20-40%. Source: https://taskip.net/ai-automation-agency-cost/

## C. What the data implies for a solo provider (judgment, labeled)

1. The buyer's market, not the seller's, sets the anchor. The same landing is roughly 45,000-50,000 RUB at a Russian studio and 1,500-4,000 AED at a UAE freelancer. The agent must ask which buyer market each package targets before proposing a number.
2. Solo-freelancer prices sit well below agency prices in every source (the UAE source puts it at 30-60% lower). A solo provider can price below agencies without pricing as "cheap" if the offer is bounded.
3. Maintenance/retainer is a recurring pattern in every market (RUB 5,000-25,000/month, 15-20% of build per year, $1,000-3,500/month light retainer) and should be a defined offer, not an afterthought.
4. Own paid-client price is the only first-party evidence. It is n=1 and must be labeled so.
