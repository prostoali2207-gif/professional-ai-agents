## Competency delta

### D1 — BN-03/04: independent divergence and anti-anchoring

Add:
- user-suggested names/territories are candidate evidence, not search-space locks;
- for open briefs, preserve at least one materially different counter-territory until convergence is justified;
- do not let screening happen so early that it suppresses creative exploration;
- divergence must differ by semantic mechanism, identity mode, tonality, construction, or narrative device—not only wording.

### D2 — NEW BN-19: Naturalness, authenticity, and contrivance judgment

Observable capability:
- distinguishes useful distinctiveness from conspicuous “look how clever/unusual this is” construction;
- evaluates whether the name sounds natural in the target language and authentic to the approved identity;
- recognizes when the rationale is doing more work than the name;
- preserves unusual names when the unusualness feels coherent rather than decorative.

Failure modes:
- novelty as default proxy for quality;
- paradox/micro-sentence gimmick repetition;
- over-designed coinages whose only defense is explanation;
- rejecting all unusual names in the name of naturalness.

This is judgment, not a hard numeric score.

### D3 — NEW BN-20: Identity-mode and first-read classification

For personal-brand work, identify the intended/acceptable identity mode:
- name-led;
- person-alias;
- concept-led.

Test the candidate's likely first read:
- person/alias;
- role/title;
- place/venue;
- project/product;
- company/studio/community;
- phrase/state/other.

A mismatch is:
`FATAL | MATERIAL | TOLERABLE | INTENTIONAL`
depending on the brief.

Do not assume every personal brand must contain the person's name, and do not assume every concept-led handle must read like a person.

### D4 — BN-08/10: collision relevance model

Before treating an observed use as a material collision, classify:
- similarity: exact / phonetic / semantic / visual / commercial-impression;
- usage type: ordinary phrase / title / handle / company / product / trademark;
- audience/category relationship;
- channel overlap;
- prominence/likelihood of identity confusion.

Operational tiers:
- T0 semantic/search noise;
- T1 unrelated title/phrase use;
- T2 adjacent identity signal;
- T3 material identity collision;
- T4 legal/high-risk escalation.

The tiers are naming triage, not legal opinions.

### D5 — BN-07/13: mandatory oral/typed/segmentation gate

Before ranking a serious finalist:
1. say/read it as ordinary speech;
2. generate plausible listener spellings;
3. inspect homophones and alternate word boundaries;
4. inspect lowercase handle segmentation;
5. test likely first meanings/polysemy;
6. record material ambiguity.

A candidate cannot rank first while a material unrecorded transcription defect remains.

### D6 — BN-09/14: discovery-path weighting

For personal handles, define likely discovery paths:
- in-app tap/recommendation;
- display-name search;
- exact handle search;
- spoken referral;
- external web search;
- direct link/QR.

Weight findability/uniqueness against the actual path. Do not import company-domain/SEO logic as a universal hard rule.

### D7 — NEW BN-21: comparative consistency / metamorphic check

Before final recommendation:
- identify the rule used to penalize/advance each finalist;
- test whether the same rule would apply to a near-equivalent candidate with an irrelevant variable changed;
- if the outcome flips, state the decision-relevant variable or correct the inconsistency.

### D8 — NEW BN-22: Decision economy and anti-ceremonial judgment

Observable capability:
- distinguishes material naming objections from ceremonial caveats;
- does not manufacture balancing objections merely to appear rigorous, independent or senior;
- accepts a simple/conventional candidate when it is strongest after real exploration;
- stops divergence/research when additional search has low expected decision value;
- keeps recommendation rationale proportional to the decision.

Failure modes:
- over-explaining weak distinctions;
- adding caveats that do not change the choice or required verification;
- endless exploration after a clear winner exists;
- treating complexity as professionalism;
- making a simple strong name seem inferior because it has less story to explain.

Evidence basis:
- `docs/runtime-judgment-and-opportunity.md`: objection materiality gate, simplicity is not amateurism, novelty is not quality, converge when additional search has low expected decision value, and do not reward verbosity/caveat count;
- `architect/methodology/creative-profession-architecture.md`: overformalization and novelty that harms function are explicit creative failure modes.


### D9 — NEW BN-23: Magnetism / evocative charge without contrivance

Observable capability:
- distinguishes clean-but-forgettable naming from naming with genuine pull, character, atmosphere or memorable identity charge;
- detects when anti-contrivance has collapsed the solution into generic safe naming;
- preserves candidates that feel vivid or ownable without requiring a clever explanation;
- can prefer a simple vivid name over both a bland safe name and an over-designed clever name.

Decision model:
- **bland**: correct/natural but low recall, low character, little identity signal;
- **magnetic**: memorable/evocative with coherent identity fit and low explanation burden;
- **contrived**: unusualness or cleverness is conspicuously engineered and carries explanation burden.

Do not use “magnetism” as an unexplained taste label. Judge through:
- immediate memorability;
- tonal/personality signal;
- evocative imagery or attitude when appropriate;
- rhythm/sound/word-form;
- whether the candidate still has appeal with rationale removed;
- distinctiveness relative to relevant alternatives.

Failure modes:
- safe generic winner;
- novelty theatre;
- overcorrecting every atmospheric/abstract option into name + work/function syntax;
- using decision economy to terminate before a vivid viable territory has been tested.

Evidence basis:
- v0.1 evidence E05: Catchword includes magnetism among naming trade-off criteria;
- Agent Architect creative methodology treats generic polished output as a failure and requires originality plus appropriateness;
- production evidence F12 shows the anti-contrivance repair can itself become a bias.
