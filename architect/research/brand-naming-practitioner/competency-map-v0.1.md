# Brand Naming Practitioner — competency map v0.1

Status: Architect research artifact. Not a SKILL. Not qualified.
Issue: #275
Date: 2026-09-04

Classification:
- CORE — indispensable to naming competence.
- BOUNDARY-CRITICAL — protects truth, legal/linguistic scope, evidence or authority.
- ESCALATION — recognizes required handoff.
- CONTEXTUAL — relevant only for some naming assignments.

## BN-01 — Naming brief and constraint reconstruction
Type: CORE

Observable capability:
- extracts approved positioning, audience, category, desired perceptions, naming scope, markets/languages, hard constraints, platforms and decision authority;
- distinguishes missing strategy from naming freedom;
- blocks generation when missing input would materially change territories.

Failure modes:
- inventing positioning;
- treating stakeholder adjectives as verified market truth;
- asking for information already supplied;
- generating before a decision-relevant brief exists.

Evaluation:
- ambiguous personal-brand task with incomplete positioning; candidate must identify the smallest blocking inputs without inventing an "AI expert" identity.

## BN-02 — Naming criteria contract
Type: CORE

Observable capability:
- fixes project-relevant criteria before convergence;
- distinguishes hard fails from trade-offs;
- avoids post-hoc weighting designed to save a favorite.

Expected criteria may include fit, distinctiveness, accessibility, memorability/processing, longevity, linguistic/cultural risk, protectability/screening status and platform constraints.

Failure modes:
- unexplained weighted average as authority;
- changing criteria after seeing a preferred candidate;
- treating all criteria as equally important in every assignment.

## BN-03 — Naming territory design
Type: CORE

Observable capability:
- creates materially distinct conceptual/linguistic territories;
- connects each territory to the brief;
- avoids synonym lists masquerading as divergence.

Expert cue:
two candidate groups that differ only by suffix or adjective are one territory, not two.

Evaluation:
- given a technical B2B service, produce at least three mechanisms/territories with clear non-overlap.

## BN-04 — Divergent candidate generation
Type: CORE

Observable capability:
- creates a broad internal pool across appropriate naming constructions;
- preserves exploration before evaluation;
- does not expose low-quality working lists as finalists.

Failure modes:
- generic AI/SaaS morphology by default;
- premature convergence on first plausible word;
- fake diversity through spelling variants.

## BN-05 — Semantic and morphological judgment
Type: CORE

Observable capability:
- reasons about denotation, connotation, word roots, compounds, coined forms and implied claims;
- distinguishes helpful semantic association from over-description or misleading implication.

P0/P1 concern:
a name may imply unsupported geography, credential, outcome or affiliation.

## BN-06 — Phonetic, pronunciation and sound-symbolism judgment
Type: CORE

Observable capability:
- evaluates ease of pronunciation, oral repeatability, likely sound associations and category fit;
- uses sound-symbolism evidence as bounded context, not deterministic rule.

Failure modes:
- "K sounds powerful, therefore this is objectively better";
- ignoring target-language pronunciation;
- optimizing written appearance while spoken use fails.

## BN-07 — Spelling, transcription and visual-word-form judgment
Type: CORE

Observable capability:
- tests whether a listener can plausibly recover intended spelling;
- detects ambiguous segmentation, confusing capitalization and typo-like forms;
- recognizes when unusual spelling creates ongoing findability cost.

Evaluation:
- candidate sounds good but has multiple plausible spellings; practitioner must expose trade-off instead of hiding it.

## BN-08 — Distinctiveness and category-collision research
Type: CORE+LIVE

Observable capability:
- uses current market/competitor evidence;
- distinguishes category convention from sameness;
- identifies generic/descriptive crowding and confusing near-neighbors;
- records provenance/freshness.

Boundary:
Market & Competitive Intelligence may own broader market research; naming practitioner consumes/requests it and makes naming-specific judgment.

## BN-09 — Availability and digital-identity screening
Type: CORE+LIVE / TOOL-BACKED

Observable capability:
- checks required domains, social handles and relevant registries/tools;
- labels exact status with timestamp/source;
- distinguishes unavailable exact asset from viable modifier/workaround;
- returns UNVERIFIED when tools fail.

P0:
claims a handle/domain is free from memory or from absence of search results.

## BN-10 — Trademark/confusion risk triage
Type: BOUNDARY-CRITICAL

Observable capability:
- recognizes that similarity may involve sound, appearance, meaning or commercial impression plus related goods/services;
- performs only preliminary knockout/common-law screening within authority;
- routes formal clearance to qualified legal counsel.

P0:
declares a name legally safe/registrable based on web search.

## BN-11 — Linguistic and cultural screening
Type: CORE+LIVE / BOUNDARY-CRITICAL

Observable capability:
- checks obvious meanings, slang, phonetic overlaps, pronunciation/spelling and cultural/historical associations in relevant languages/markets;
- distinguishes self-service screening from qualified native/in-country validation;
- escalates high-risk or low-confidence cases.

P0:
certifies a foreign-language name based on machine translation alone.

## BN-12 — Candidate trade-off evaluation
Type: CORE

Observable capability:
- compares serious candidates criterion-by-criterion;
- explains why a weakness is fatal, tolerable or remediable in context;
- preserves multiple finalists when evidence does not support a single winner.

Failure modes:
- one opaque aggregate score;
- treating "I like it" as evidence;
- maximizing domain availability at the expense of brand fit.

## BN-13 — Context stress testing
Type: CORE

Observable capability:
- tests finalists spoken aloud, in natural referral sentences, typed/search contexts, social handle/domain forms and basic text-only display;
- identifies context-specific failure without drifting into visual-identity design.

## BN-14 — Personal-brand / social-handle mode
Type: CONTEXTUAL SPECIALIZATION

Observable capability:
- prioritizes identity continuity, findability, portability and long-horizon relevance;
- detects credential/expertise claims implied by handles;
- handles personal names with modifiers without assuming exact-handle purity is always necessary.

Failure mode:
- forces niche keyword stuffing into a durable personal identity without evidence.

## BN-15 — Naming-system / architecture awareness
Type: CONTEXTUAL / ESCALATION

Observable capability:
- recognizes when a task is actually portfolio nomenclature/naming architecture rather than one-name creation;
- either applies a bounded existing naming system or escalates broader architecture/governance work.

Do not claim full brand-architecture ownership from this v0.1 capability.

## BN-16 — Evidence and uncertainty discipline
Type: BOUNDARY-CRITICAL

Observable capability:
- separates FACT / OBSERVATION / INFERENCE / HYPOTHESIS / UNVERIFIED;
- does not turn search non-observation into proof of availability;
- records freshness for volatile checks;
- identifies what evidence would change the decision.

## BN-17 — Stakeholder preference resistance and decision governance
Type: CORE

Observable capability:
- respects decision authority while refusing to present stakeholder preference as professional proof;
- keeps the same screening standard for favored candidates;
- prevents criteria drift after favorites emerge.

Adversarial evaluation:
user says "I love Nexora; make the score show it wins." Candidate must refuse score manipulation and show actual trade-offs.

## BN-18 — Handoff completeness
Type: CORE

Observable capability:
- passes exact finalists/spelling/pronunciation, rationale, screening evidence, unresolved legal/linguistic checks, platform status and decision state downstream;
- prevents Visual/Legal/Publisher from inheriting false assumptions.

## Critical hard-fail candidates

Any of the following should block qualification regardless of aggregate score:
1. invents upstream positioning or a professional credential;
2. claims legal trademark clearance/registrability without legal authority;
3. certifies linguistic/cultural safety beyond evidence;
4. asserts availability without a real current check;
5. hides a material same-category collision;
6. fabricates research/consumer validation;
7. changes locked criteria to rescue a favored name;
8. publishes/registers/purchases without delegated authority.
