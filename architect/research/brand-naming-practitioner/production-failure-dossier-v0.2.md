# Brand Naming Practitioner v0.2 — production-failure dossier

Status: Agent Architect research artifact. Not a SKILL. Not qualified.
Issue: #282
Date: 2026-09-05

## Purpose

Convert repeated applied naming corrections into professional repair evidence for a materially revised Brand Naming Practitioner candidate.

This dossier does **not** reinterpret the frozen v0.1 qualification cycle. v0.1 remains terminal `NOT_EXECUTABLE` with no professional PASS/REVISE/FAIL verdict.

Applied corrections are production observations, not automatic professional truth. They become repair evidence only where they expose a repeatable decision defect and/or align with external professional evidence.

## Source boundary

Primary production traces:
- Personal Brand Growth System naming decision history, especially PBGS-INSTAGRAM-HANDLE-001..005;
- the applied 2026-09-04/05 naming conversation that produced and revised candidates including `prostoali`, `AliOperator`, `Probably Deliberate`, `Past The Last Door`, `No Sign Outside`, `Still Curing`, and related variants;
- user corrections, Claude critique, and subsequent assistant/core review.

Not every preference change is a professional failure. The relevant signal is a **repeated missed criterion, inconsistent rule application, or weak creative mechanism** that a strong naming practitioner should have surfaced before the user did.

## Failure taxonomy

### F01 — Premature anchoring / territory capture

Observed:
- early exploration repeatedly anchored on `prostoali`;
- later, user suggestions such as “place / room / abstract phrase” became the dominant search frame instead of one hypothesis among several;
- materially different territories often appeared only after external prompting.

Impacted:
- BN-03 territory design;
- BN-04 divergent generation;
- BN-17 preference resistance.

Root-cause layer:
- judgment + workflow + evaluation.

Repair hypothesis:
- when an open brief remains, preserve at least one materially different counter-territory before convergence;
- treat user-supplied territories as hypotheses, not hidden locks;
- separate divergence from screening so caution does not suppress exploration.

### F02 — Role/title mistaken for personal identity

Observed:
- `AliOperator` survived too long despite reading more like a function/title/persona than a durable personal alias.

Impacted:
- BN-05 semantics;
- BN-14 personal-brand mode.

Root-cause layer:
- personal-brand specialization was too shallow.

Repair hypothesis:
- explicitly test first-read identity type: person/alias, title/role, place/venue, project/product, company/studio, phrase/state;
- compare that first read with the intended identity architecture.

### F03 — Performative cleverness mistaken for creative quality

Observed:
- pools such as `Probably Deliberate`, `This Seemed Reasonable`, `More Or Less Me`, `Usually Elsewhere` were distinctive but increasingly felt visibly “designed to seem unusual”;
- explanation quality often carried more charm than the actual handle.

Impacted:
- BN-04 divergent generation;
- BN-12 trade-off judgment;
- creative-quality model generally.

Root-cause layer:
- missing naturalness/authenticity/contrivance judgment.

Repair hypothesis:
- add explicit comparison of naturalness, authenticity-to-brief, and “effort visibility” / performative cleverness;
- novelty cannot compensate for a name that feels self-consciously constructed;
- this is a trade-off dimension, not a universal ban on unusual names.

### F04 — Place/project identity misread in personal-brand mode

Observed:
- `No Sign Outside` was promoted as primary without surfacing that its first read can be an unmarked venue, room, studio, project, or community rather than a person.

Impacted:
- BN-14 personal-brand mode;
- BN-13 context stress test.

Root-cause layer:
- missing identity-type first-read test.

Repair hypothesis:
- personal-brand work must distinguish at least:
  - name-led identity;
  - person-alias identity;
  - concept-led identity;
- if the brief wants the handle itself to feel person-like, place/project ambiguity becomes material;
- if concept-led identity is explicitly desired, the same ambiguity may be tolerable.

### F05 — Collision relevance over-penalized

Observed:
- unrelated safety terminology, cosmetics/coatings usage, restaurants/venues, music/book titles, and descriptive phrase usage were sometimes treated too much like competitive identity collisions;
- `Low Visibility Room`, `Under The Primer`, `Service Entrance`, and similar cases exposed the problem.

Impacted:
- BN-08 distinctiveness/collision;
- BN-10 confusion triage;
- BN-12 trade-off evaluation.

Root-cause layer:
- collision model lacked relevance tiers.

Repair hypothesis:
separate:
1. ordinary semantic/search noise;
2. unrelated title/phrase use;
3. adjacent public identity with overlapping audience/channel;
4. same/related category identity with plausible source confusion;
5. legal/trademark risk requiring specialist review.

Search presence alone is not collision.

### F06 — Inconsistent application of collision logic

Observed:
- `After The Last Door` was rejected for a 2026 music-title use while semantically near-equivalent `Past The Last Door` was simultaneously promoted.

Impacted:
- BN-12;
- BN-16 evidence discipline.

Root-cause layer:
- missing contrastive consistency check.

Repair hypothesis:
- before final ranking, run a metamorphic consistency pass: if only an irrelevant surface variable changes, the professional rule should not flip without an explicit reason.

### F07 — Spoken-to-written transcription defect missed

Observed:
- `past` / `passed` homophony was not caught before `pastthelastdoor` became primary;
- lowercase segmentation around `...stthe...` was also missed.

Impacted:
- BN-07 spelling/transcription;
- BN-13 context stress test.

Root-cause layer:
- competency existed in v0.1 but workflow/evaluation did not force execution strongly enough.

Repair hypothesis:
- mandatory pre-ranking oral-to-handle pass:
  - homophones;
  - plausible alternate spellings;
  - word-boundary ambiguity;
  - all-lowercase segmentation;
  - read-back from audio to typed handle.

### F08 — Polysemy / first-meaning ambiguity underweighted

Observed:
- `Half Lit Landing` carried a strong intended stair-landing scene, but `landing` can first parse as aircraft landing or web landing page.

Impacted:
- BN-05 semantics;
- BN-13 context test.

Repair hypothesis:
- test likely first meaning, not only intended meaning;
- ambiguity is not automatically fatal, but must be surfaced before rank.

### F09 — Company-naming findability criteria over-transferred to personal handles

Observed:
- web/search uniqueness was sometimes used too strongly against personal-handle candidates whose actual discovery can be by tap, display name, profile link, or platform context.

Impacted:
- BN-09 digital identity;
- BN-14 personal-brand mode.

Root-cause layer:
- context weighting missing.

Repair hypothesis:
- define expected discovery paths before weighting exact-web uniqueness;
- spoken referral and typing remain important when relevant;
- company/product SEO/ownership logic must not silently become a universal personal-handle rule.

### F10 — Creative divergence depended too much on external challenger

Observed:
- the strongest territory expansion occurred only after composing with qualified Social Content Creative.

Interpretation:
- composition was useful, but a naming practitioner should still possess strong naming-specific divergence on its own.

Root-cause layer:
- BN-03/04 creative craft under-modeled/evaluated.

Repair hypothesis:
- v0.2 must demonstrate standalone naming divergence;
- Social Content Creative may remain an optional applied challenger, not a required crutch and not a naming owner.

## Non-agent failure explicitly excluded

Cross-chat confusion about the current selected handle was traced to the applied repository lacking a canonical current-identity source-of-truth record. That was an applied state-management defect, not Brand Naming Practitioner professional judgment, and has been fixed downstream.

## Repair priority

P1 professional repair:
1. anti-anchoring + real divergence;
2. naturalness/authenticity vs performative cleverness;
3. personal identity-type first read;
4. context-sensitive collision relevance;
5. mandatory transcription/segmentation/polysemy gate;
6. consistency/metamorphic comparison.

P2:
- context-weighted personal-handle discoverability;
- optional creative-challenger composition.

### F11 — Ceremonial over-analysis / decision drag

Observed:
- naming reviews repeatedly accumulated low-value objections, long rationales and extra distinctions after the decision-relevant trade-offs were already clear;
- weak candidates could appear more sophisticated because the explanation was elaborate;
- the process sometimes kept exploring after additional search had low plausible decision value.

Impacted:
- BN-12 shortlist judgment;
- BN-18 handoff/output discipline;
- runtime convergence behavior.

Root-cause layer:
- judgment + output discipline.

Repair hypothesis:
- apply a materiality gate to caveats: surface an objection only when it can change selection, verification, implementation, risk or handoff;
- prefer the simplest strong candidate that satisfies the brief; simplicity is not amateurism;
- stop exploring when additional search has low expected decision value;
- do not use explanation volume, caveat count or number of alternatives as evidence of professionalism;
- a strong naming recommendation should normally be understandable without a defensive essay.
