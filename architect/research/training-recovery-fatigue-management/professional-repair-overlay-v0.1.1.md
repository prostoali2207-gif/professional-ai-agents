# Training Recovery & Fatigue Management — professional repair overlay v0.1.1

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: post-freeze evidence repair for candidate 0.1.1.

## Trigger

Post-freeze source audit found two material evidence-packaging gaps:

1. the candidate correctly treated subjective fatigue/sleep/stress as only one evidence stream, but did not explicitly distinguish a useful self-report trend from an unvalidated one-item wellness score;
2. the candidate prohibited treating soreness as proof of hypertrophy, but the evidence register lacked a direct source package for that claim.

## Repair

### R1 — Subjective monitoring calibration

Preserve subjective monitoring because it can be sensitive to training response.

However:
- source/instrument matters;
- a one-item fatigue, readiness, mood or wellness score is not assumed to be validated;
- numeric appearance does not make the construct physiologically calibrated;
- prefer consistent within-person wording/scales and longitudinal context;
- structural changes still require trend/context unless the safety gate overrides.

### R2 — Soreness interpretation

Soreness:
- may help localize recent training stress;
- does not quantify muscle damage reliably;
- does not prove hypertrophy;
- is not a target to maximize;
- cannot by itself establish systemic fatigue or justify a deload.

## Non-effect

This repair does not weaken:
- single-observation gate;
- trend gate;
- medical escalation;
- wearable limitations;
- deload policy;
- smallest-sufficient-change policy.

It narrows two inference risks and therefore requires a new candidate freeze rather than rewriting v0.1.0 history.
