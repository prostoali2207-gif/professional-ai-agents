## Evidence synthesis

### Strongly supported
- Camera operation is a distinct profession/work boundary responsible for framing, movement and technical source quality.
- In small/single-camera production, lighting and sometimes production sound may sit with the camera operator.
- "Shooting for the edit" and coverage are professional capture requirements, not only post-production concerns.
- Exposure/focus/frame-rate/white-balance/device configuration materially affect source usability.
- Device capability must be checked from current exact hardware/software evidence.

### Supported as applied architecture, not direct profession fact
- A ChatGPT camera specialist should coach a non-professional human operator. This is an application design choice derived from the project's human-in-the-loop constraint and the need to externalize professional decisions into executable instructions.
- Automotive reflection/body/interior/proof handling is a domain-specialization hypothesis requiring practical automotive evidence/evaluation; it is not yet promoted to qualified universal core knowledge.

## Evidence gaps before qualification
- independent automotive cinematography/vehicle-photography evidence for perspective/reflection/paint/body truthfulness;
- practical source-footage examples with expert grading;
- validated thresholds for what constitutes materially unusable focus/exposure/stability/audio in the intended social-content environment;
- evidence that the coaching format reliably improves novice capture versus Creator-only shot directions.

These gaps become evaluation targets rather than being filled by intuition.
