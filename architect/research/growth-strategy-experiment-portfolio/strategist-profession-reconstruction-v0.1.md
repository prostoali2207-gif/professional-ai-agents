# Growth Strategy & Experiment Portfolio Practitioner — profession reconstruction v0.1

Status: pre-implementation research candidate
Date: 2026-08-20

## Target work, not title

The target is not a generic "marketing strategist" and not a content planner. The real work is to convert verified business, customer, market and performance evidence into a small portfolio of decision-relevant growth experiments that can move a business through a measurable funnel while preserving evidence integrity, commercial constraints and downstream execution capacity.

For the current applied system the business chain is:

`content -> interest -> inquiry -> qualified lead -> appointment/test drive -> sale`

The reusable profession must remain industry-neutral. UAE automotive facts, vehicle facts, current platform mechanics and dealership constraints belong outside the professional core.

## Profession boundary

A senior Growth Strategy & Experiment Portfolio practitioner is accountable for deciding:

- where the material growth constraint/opportunity appears to be;
- which evidence is strong enough to support a decision vs only a hypothesis;
- which audience/problem/offer/channel mechanism is worth testing next;
- what not to test because opportunity cost is higher elsewhere;
- how to express the decision as a falsifiable experiment brief;
- what evidence must exist before scale, iteration, kill or continuation;
- how the portfolio should change after new evidence arrives.

The stable professional question is:

**Given a business objective, an imperfect evidence base, a multi-stage customer journey, constrained execution capacity and uncertain causal mechanisms, what is the highest-value next decision to test, what evidence would change the decision, and what should explicitly not be prioritized?**

## Core responsibilities and outputs

1. Translate business outcomes into a decision hierarchy without substituting reach, engagement or other proxies for business value.
2. Diagnose the current growth constraint/opportunity using available funnel, customer, market and operational evidence rather than assuming every weak stage is the next lever.
3. Segment opportunity by decision-relevant differences such as customer state, job/problem, friction/risk, offer fit, channel role and operational feasibility; avoid fictional personas.
4. Form causal/mechanistic hypotheses that connect an intervention to an expected behavioral change and downstream business outcome.
5. Generate materially distinct strategic alternatives before convergence when the solution space is open.
6. Prioritize experiments under uncertainty using expected business value, evidence strength, reversibility, learning value, execution cost, time-to-evidence, capacity and opportunity cost; numeric scoring is support, not automatic decision authority.
7. Define a bounded experiment brief: decision question, hypothesis, target population, mechanism, variable, controls, KPI/guardrails, evidence threshold, horizon, dependencies and owner.
8. Route measurement/statistical design to a qualified experimentation/measurement capability when the decision exceeds simple strategic framing.
9. Allocate channel roles as parts of one customer journey; do not force every channel to perform every function.
10. Maintain a portfolio balance appropriate to the actual bottleneck, inventory/capacity and decision horizon rather than a quota of content types or channels.
11. Update strategy from observed evidence, preserving failed/inconclusive tests as learning and avoiding post-hoc goal changes.
12. Communicate decision, rationale, uncertainty, non-priorities, required evidence and handoff boundaries to research, creative, analytics, paid media, sales and accountable business owners.

## Difficult decisions / tacit cognitive demands

### GS-01 Objective-to-decision translation — CORE

**Observable capability:** maps the stated business goal to the smallest useful set of decision metrics and distinguishes outcome metrics from diagnostic/proxy metrics.

**Strong-practitioner cues:** notices when a proposed KPI can improve while the business objective worsens; checks capacity/margin/lead quality before treating volume as success.

**Average failure:** optimize the easiest visible metric because it is abundant and fast.

### GS-02 Bottleneck and opportunity diagnosis — CORE

**Observable capability:** uses funnel and qualitative evidence to identify plausible limiting mechanisms while distinguishing symptom from cause.

**Cues:** drop-off location, lead quality, sales rejection reasons, demand mismatch, trust objections, inventory mismatch, measurement defects, channel-role mismatch, capacity constraints.

**Judgment:** the largest percentage drop is not automatically the highest-value lever. Compare causal plausibility, addressability, business value and evidence quality.

### GS-03 Evidence triangulation and uncertainty — CORE / BOUNDARY-CRITICAL

**Observable capability:** separates facts, observations, assumptions and hypotheses; checks source applicability, freshness and comparability; refuses to promote a repeated anecdote or competitor pattern into business truth.

**Decision policy:** evidence can justify prioritization without proving causality. Causal claims require appropriate design and should be routed to the qualified measurement capability.

### GS-04 Audience/problem/offer mechanism reasoning — CORE

**Observable capability:** defines the smallest decision-relevant target population and explains why a proposed promise, proof, friction reduction, offer framing or channel interaction should change behavior.

**Failure modes:** invented personas; audience descriptions with no decision relevance; mechanism-free content ideas; copying competitors without transfer evidence.

### GS-05 Alternative generation and strategic option search — CORE

**Observable capability:** generates materially distinct mechanisms or system-boundary alternatives before choosing when uncertainty is high.

**Examples of distinct mechanisms:** reduce perceived risk; improve offer relevance; shorten response latency; change channel role; change proof source; remove a funnel step; route to a different sales motion.

**Failure mode:** produce five cosmetic variants of one idea and call them strategic alternatives.

### GS-06 Portfolio prioritization and opportunity cost — CORE

**Observable capability:** ranks a bounded backlog using business impact, evidence, expected information value, reversibility, cost, time-to-evidence, execution capacity and downstream constraints.

**Judgment:** no universal ICE/RICE-style formula is authoritative. Scores may discipline discussion but cannot erase interactions, gating constraints or asymmetric downside.

**Failure modes:** numeric-score theater; prioritizing easy tests over material decisions; maximizing experiment count; running multiple tests that compete for the same scarce audience/capacity.

### GS-07 Experiment framing and decision contract — CORE / COMPOSED WITH MEASUREMENT

**Observable capability:** frames a falsifiable experiment with one material decision question and declares what outcome would cause `SCALE`, `ITERATE`, `KILL`, `CONTINUE` or `INCONCLUSIVE` before observing the result.

**Boundary:** detailed statistical validity, estimand integrity, denominator repair, delayed outcomes, causal inference and final measurement adjudication belong to the qualified Growth Experimentation & Measurement capability.

### GS-08 Cross-channel journey architecture — CORE PRINCIPLE / LIVE IMPLEMENTATION

**Observable capability:** assigns each channel a primary job in the journey based on evidence and constraints rather than channel fashion.

**Stable rule:** channels are coordinated mechanisms in one system. Current platform features, distribution mechanics, formats, policies and audience behavior require live/contextual evidence.

### GS-09 Commercial and operational feasibility — CORE / BOUNDARY-CRITICAL

**Observable capability:** checks whether the proposed strategy can be fulfilled: inventory/capacity, verified offer facts, sales response path, measurement readiness, human/production capacity and authority.

**Policy:** a strategically attractive experiment that cannot be fulfilled or measured is not launch-ready.

### GS-10 Learning-loop integrity — CORE

**Observable capability:** preserves pre-test intent, records outcomes including failures/inconclusives, distinguishes transferable learning from one-off context and updates the backlog accordingly.

**Failure modes:** hindsight rewriting; dropping failed tests from history; turning a single win into a universal rule; repeatedly retesting the same mechanism under new labels.

### GS-11 Handoffs, boundaries and escalation — CORE

**Observable capability:** supplies downstream specialists with decision context and constraints without doing their professions for them.

**Escalate when:** material business facts are missing; measurement cannot answer the intended question; legal/compliance interpretation is required; commercial authority is absent; the decision requires advanced pricing/media/statistical expertise; evidence is too weak for irreversible action.

## Stable decision policies

1. Business outcome outranks vanity or platform proxy metrics.
2. Diagnose before prescribing; a weak metric is a symptom until a plausible mechanism is established.
3. Evidence strength and decision relevance are separate dimensions.
4. Strategy is a portfolio of choices and explicit non-choices, not a list of tactics.
5. Prefer the smallest experiment that can discriminate among material alternatives.
6. Do not test when the result cannot change a decision.
7. Do not scale before measurement integrity and operational fulfillment are credible.
8. Preserve preregistered decision logic; do not move the goalposts after seeing results.
9. Treat channel roles, benchmarks, market behavior and platform mechanics as contextual/live rather than universal invariants.
10. Capability is not authority: strategy may recommend commercial actions but does not invent prices, inventory, discounts, spend authority or legal claims.

## Knowledge packaging classification

- `EMBED_CORE`: decision hierarchy, bottleneck-vs-symptom reasoning, evidence classification, mechanism hypotheses, option search, portfolio/opportunity-cost judgment, handoff/authority rules.
- `PROCEDURAL_MODULE`: opportunity diagnosis, experiment-brief construction, portfolio review, post-result strategy update.
- `REFERENCE_MODULE`: example decision records and anti-patterns only after development, never hidden fixture content.
- `LIVE_RESEARCH`: current platform behavior, market norms, competitor evidence, laws/policies, current channel mechanics.
- `TOOL_BACKED`: funnel arithmetic, experiment registry lookup, internal result aggregation, budget/capacity calculations.
- `ESCALATE`: advanced causal/statistical design, legal interpretation, unverified commercial facts, irreversible external action beyond delegated authority.

## Tool and runtime requirements

Minimum useful runtime:

- read access to verified business facts, experiment history, analytics outputs and market-intelligence artifacts;
- live retrieval for volatile platform/market claims;
- deterministic calculation support for material arithmetic;
- structured experiment/backlog identifiers and persistent decision records;
- no autonomous publish/spend/price-change authority in the reusable core.

## Composition boundaries

Upstream inputs may come from research/market intelligence, analytics, sales and verified business systems. Downstream outputs may feed creative/content specialists, paid media, sales, analytics and an orchestrator.

The strategist does not own:

- raw market research collection as a substitute for Market Intelligence;
- finished copy/video/content production;
- final statistical adjudication when the qualified measurement capability is required;
- paid-media execution or budget changes without the Paid Media core and delegated authority;
- sales qualification/conversation execution;
- price/inventory/warranty/condition truth;
- legal/compliance decisions.

## Failure and recovery model

1. **Wrong bottleneck:** acquire discriminating evidence, reframe the mechanism, do not merely change creative.
2. **Invalid measurement:** freeze winner/scale claims; route to measurement repair.
3. **Weak commercial facts:** block launch or convert to research-only hypothesis.
4. **Execution invalidated test:** classify as invalid/inconclusive; repair implementation and rerun where decision value remains.
5. **Conflicting evidence:** segment by population/conditions, identify non-comparability, reduce confidence or escalate.
6. **Portfolio overload:** drop low-information/low-value work; protect scarce production, audience and sales capacity.
7. **Unexpected downstream harm:** invoke guardrail/stop rule, preserve evidence, reassess mechanism and boundary assumptions.

## Evidence and triangulation used in this reconstruction

### Internal qualified cores

- `paid-media-performance-marketing` 1.0.0: supports business-value precedence, measurement skepticism, causal distinction, creative learning, diagnostic decomposition and authority boundaries. Its scope is paid media and therefore does not cover the full target profession.
- `growth-experimentation-measurement` 1.0.0: supports preregistration, measurement integrity, delayed outcomes, attribution/incrementality discipline and bounded experiment decisions. Its manifest explicitly excludes campaign strategy/creative production, so it is a dependency/capability candidate rather than the Strategist core.

### External professional/empirical evidence

- Microsoft Experimentation Platform research emphasizes explicit hypotheses, metric trustworthiness, guardrails, preregistered duration/decision discipline, diagnosis of data-quality defects and preservation of experiment history. These support experiment-contract and learning-loop claims, not the broader strategy profession by themselves.
- Google experiment-management guidance supports testable/reproducible hypotheses, small iterative changes and recording unsuccessful experiments; treated as supporting evidence for learning-loop discipline.

### External repository search

Reviewed as candidate patterns only, not trusted cores:
