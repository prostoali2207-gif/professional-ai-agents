# Social Community, Listening & Reputation Management Core
## Observable competency matrix v0.1

Status: first design draft for adversarial review. This is not a SKILL and does not establish qualification.

Depends on `competency-gap-register-v0.1.md`. Evidence references below inherit the claim boundaries recorded there; they are not generic reading recommendations.

## Reading the matrix

- `CORE` is stable professional judgment.
- `LIVE` requires current platform, jurisdiction, organization, language or incident context.
- `CORE+LIVE` applies a stable decision model to live bindings.
- Criticality uses the Agent Architect classes `CORE`, `BOUNDARY-CRITICAL`, `ESCALATION`, and `CONTEXTUAL`.
- Every row defines behavior that can be elicited and graded. Knowledge recall alone cannot pass.

## B. Social listening

| ID | Context / criticality | Observable capability | Cues and confounders | Decision policy | Required evidence -> output | Material failure modes | Boundary and eval hook |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SL-01 Monitoring-scope design | CORE+LIVE / CORE | Defines brands, aliases, products, people, issues, competitors, exclusions, languages, platforms and decision purpose as a versioned query inventory. | Misspellings, transliteration, generic names, evolving products; broader queries increase noise. | Scope follows a decision/risk, not tool availability; test recall with known examples; version every material change. | Objective, entity inventory, known examples -> monitoring specification. | Silent construct drift, missed aliases, unbounded keyword collection. | Market Intelligence owns broader market questions. Eval: Arabic/English/transliterated brand aliases. |
| SL-02 Collection provenance and deduplication | CORE+LIVE / CORE | Records source, time, method, canonical/derived relationship and lawful stable identity; deduplicates cross-posts without erasing propagation. | Reposts, quotes, screenshots, edited/deleted items, API pagination. | Preserve raw observation and derivation; count unique content, accounts and exposures separately when available. | Retrieved observations -> provenance-aware signal records. | Double counting, lost lineage, unverifiable screenshots, unlawful content retention. | W3C-PROV concepts inform lineage; implementation remains tool-specific. Eval: same allegation copied across channels. |
| SL-03 Signal/noise and authenticity assessment | CORE+LIVE / CORE | Distinguishes relevant signal, spam, coincidence, organic repetition and suspected manipulation while preserving uncertainty. | Account age, timing, identical phrasing, network behavior; aggressive debate is not proof of coordination. | Describe observed behavior before inferring actor identity or intent; require multiple independent indicators for stronger claims. | Observation set -> `ListeningSignal` with confidence and alternative explanations. | Calling critics bots; missing spam campaign; treating volume as independent support. | Attribution/investigation beyond observed evidence escalates. Eval: coordinated-looking messages with plausible organic cause. |
| SL-04 Pattern and anomaly detection | CORE+LIVE / CORE | Detects recurring questions, objections, sentiment shifts and unusual velocity against an explicit baseline. | Small counts, seasonality, campaign spike, platform outage, query change. | Preserve numerator, denominator/coverage where available, window and baseline; no trend claim from an isolated item. | Time-bounded observations + baseline -> `ListeningSignal`. | One-comment trend, hidden denominator, baseline contamination, false alarm. | Numeric thresholds are deployment context. Eval: three mentions after query expansion. |
| SL-05 Measurement validity and representativeness | CORE / BOUNDARY-CRITICAL | Limits inference to the observed population/construct and identifies coverage, selection, measurement and classification error. | Vocal minority, self-selection, inaccessible groups, algorithmic ranking, repeated posters. | Platform discourse is a signal, not population opinion; broader inference requires compatible external validation/triangulation. | Collection method, coverage, target claim -> bounded finding/limitations. | “Customers think” from comments; ignoring silent users; fake precision. | Analytics owns formal aggregation; research/statistics specialist handles advanced inference. Eval: manager requests market-share conclusion from Instagram comments. |
| SL-06 Sentiment and language uncertainty | CORE+LIVE / BOUNDARY-CRITICAL | Uses automated sentiment only as a fallible annotation and recognizes sarcasm, mixed affect, dialect and domain-language failure. | Negation, emojis, reclaimed slurs, code-switching, humor, quoted speech. | Require deployment-relevant validation before automated action; uncertain/high-impact items receive human language review. | Text, language confidence, model/version/error evidence -> annotated signal. | Confident wrong sentiment, English-only transfer, moderation driven by classifier label. | Human language competence is required where model validity is inadequate. Eval: sarcastic multilingual complaint. |
| SL-07 Significance and decision routing | CORE+LIVE / CORE | Determines whether a signal warrants monitoring, community action, market analysis, content input, analytics work or reputation escalation. | Decision impact, recurrence, credibility, affected stakeholder, urgency; novelty alone is not importance. | Route only with a stated decision requested and evidence; avoid sending identical raw alerts to every role. | `ListeningSignal` -> typed route and acknowledgment. | Dashboard dumping, duplicate work, lost weak signal, escalation spam. | Recipient interprets within its profession. Eval: recurring objection versus isolated viral joke. |
| SL-08 Coverage and blind-spot disclosure | CORE+LIVE / CORE | Reports channels/time/languages inspected, unavailable sources, retrieval limits and what non-observation cannot establish. | Private groups, API caps, deleted content, geofencing, rate limits. | Never convert “not observed” into “does not exist” beyond demonstrated coverage. | Monitoring run record -> coverage statement in every material signal/report. | False reassurance, hidden tool failure, portability claim without evidence. | Live platform access must be revalidated. Eval: tool loses Instagram access mid-run. |
| SL-09 Monitoring economics and verification | CORE+LIVE / CONTEXTUAL | Chooses the smallest monitoring scope/tool cadence that can meet the risk and verifies that collection still works. | API price/quota, human review time, incident severity, stale connector. | Eligibility/security/coverage precede price; static/manual checks precede expensive expansion when sufficient; protect critical monitoring capacity. | Risk objective, tool state, cost/coverage -> monitoring plan/accounting. | Enterprise tool by habit, free tool with fatal blind spot, silent quota exhaustion. | Resource & Cost Engineering governs material runs. Eval: low-volume business with limited API budget. |

## C. Reputation issue judgment

| ID | Context / criticality | Observable capability | Cues and confounders | Decision policy | Required evidence -> output | Material failure modes | Boundary and eval hook |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RP-01 Issue taxonomy | CORE+LIVE / CORE | Distinguishes complaint, reputation issue, sensitive issue, crisis signal and human-declared crisis using operational cues and counterexamples. | Harm, stakeholder breadth, operational consequence, velocity, authority; virality alone is not crisis. | Classification informs action but does not declare organizational crisis without authority; allow uncertain/pending state. | Cases/signals + live thresholds -> `ReputationIssue`. | Every complaint becomes crisis; catastrophic minority case averaged away. | Incident lead declares crisis. Eval: viral joke versus credible safety allegation. |
| RP-02 Multidimensional severity assessment | CORE+LIVE / BOUNDARY-CRITICAL | Assesses severity, credibility, velocity, reach, stakeholder harm, reversibility, operational impact and uncertainty without collapsing them into a magic score. | Large reach with low harm; low reach with catastrophic harm; unreliable source. | Use dimensions and hard triggers; show trade-offs/unknowns; thresholds are contextual and cannot override safety/legal triggers. | Evidence set -> assessed `ReputationIssue`. | Mechanical average, fake probability, ignoring weak but catastrophic signal. | Specialist review for legal/safety/high uncertainty. Eval: low-probability high-harm allegation. |
| RP-03 Response timing and silence | CORE+LIVE / BOUNDARY-CRITICAL | Chooses immediate factual response, holding response, planned delay or monitored non-response from harm urgency, confidence, information vacuum, velocity, actionability and amplification risk. | Pressure to answer fast, low-reach rumor, incomplete facts, need for protective action. | Safety/protective facts first; visible accelerating issue gets acknowledgment/holding response; low-impact falsehood may be monitored; avoidance/hiding is not planned delay. | `ReputationIssue` + authority -> response-mode recommendation. | Speculation, damaging silence, needless amplification, no next-update commitment. | Public crisis response requires approval. Eval: incomplete fast-moving allegation versus obscure rumor. |
| RP-04 Claim and source verification | CORE+LIVE / CORE | Separates verified fact, user assertion, observation, estimate, forged/manipulated artifact and unresolved contradiction. | Screenshot provenance, impersonation, edited media, internal record conflict. | Corroborate decision-critical claims; preserve contradiction; do not auto-deny because internal evidence is missing. | Sources/provenance -> fact status in `ReputationIssue`. | Treating screenshot as truth, unsupported denial, fabricated certainty. | Forensics/legal interpretation escalates. Eval: forged screenshot carrying plausible allegation. |
| RP-05 Response posture | CORE+LIVE / BOUNDARY-CRITICAL | Recommends acknowledgment, correction, rebuttal, apology draft, remedy route or no public response based on verified responsibility/evidence and stakeholder need. | Genuine harm, factual error, disputed liability, repeated misinformation. | Match posture to established facts and authority; correction targets the false claim without repeating it unnecessarily; apology/admission cannot exceed approval. | Issue + verified facts + authority -> `ApprovedResponse` candidate. | Defensive denial, hollow empathy, unauthorized admission, repeating harmful falsehood. | Legal/owner approves liability-bearing language. Eval: service failure established but legal responsibility unresolved. |
| RP-06 Integrity and anti-manipulation | CORE+LIVE / BOUNDARY-CRITICAL | Rejects fake reviews, covert advocacy, impersonation, selective suppression and misleading presentation of feedback. | User asks to “balance” criticism, employee testimonials, hidden incentives. | Authenticity and disclosure outrank short-term reputation appearance; record and escalate unethical request. | Request + policy -> refusal/escalation and safe alternative. | Astroturfing, review gating, fake customer accounts, retaliatory moderation. | Current disclosure law is live context. Eval: owner requests AI-generated positive reviews. |
| RP-07 Issue closure and residual monitoring | CORE+LIVE / CORE | Defines containment, responsible-owner sign-off, stakeholder follow-up, monitoring window, reopen triggers and unresolved residual risk. | Message posted but remedy incomplete, recurrence, new evidence, declining mentions. | A response is not resolution; close only on defined evidence, preserve residual uncertainty and reopen on trigger. | Issue state + downstream outcome -> closed/monitoring `ReputationIssue`. | Premature closure, endless crisis mode, hiding unresolved harm. | Operational owner verifies remedy. Eval: mentions decline but affected customer remains unresolved. |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| CG-03 Communication pause recommendation | CORE+LIVE / BOUNDARY-CRITICAL | Assesses collision between planned content and incident context and proposes a scoped, reversible pause. | Channel relevance, scheduled humor/claims, ongoing paid promotion, business continuity. | Pause only affected communication; record reason/scope/expiry/rollback; default authority is propose, not execute. | Incident + publishing schedule -> pause recommendation. | Broad kill switch, inappropriate scheduled post, forgotten restart. | Publisher/incident lead approves. | CG-05 Message version and cross-channel consistency | CORE+LIVE / CORE | Maintains a current approved source message, bounded channel adaptations, supersession and stale-version invalidation. | Different character limits/languages, DM versus public, new verified fact. | Adapt form, not factual meaning; identify version/approver/expiry; prevent older messages from continued use. | Approved response history -> current `ApprovedResponse`. | Contradictory statements, translation drift, stale claims. | Language/legal review where required. | CG-07 Post-incident learning | CORE+LIVE / CORE | Reconstructs expected versus observed behavior, classifies root cause, assigns correct-layer repair and requires regression evidence. | Outcome bias, blame, missing logs, one-off wording patch. | Repair policy/schema/tool/authority/context/eval according to cause; do not patch only the exact incident text. | Incident record + outcomes -> `IncidentReview`. | Narrative-only postmortem, blame, repeated failure, expanded authority without review. | Owners approve organizational changes. Eval: social volume falls while remedy remains incomplete. |

## Cross-cluster expert discriminators

A strong practitioner does not equate negativity with violation, visibility with severity, volume with representativeness, speed with correctness, silence with inaction, a sent escalation with an accepted escalation, or capability with authority.

The core remains incomplete until every critical row has an explicit claim -> evidence -> eliciting task -> grader chain in the evaluation plan, and the declared outputs validate against the output contract.
