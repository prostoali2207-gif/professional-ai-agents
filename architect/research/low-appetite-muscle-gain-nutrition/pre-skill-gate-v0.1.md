## Assembly constraints carried into SKILL

The SKILL must:
- optimize for minimum sufficient intake, not maximal surplus;
- never convert low food volume into chronic under-fueling;
- preserve the protein sufficiency logic and not inflate protein when energy/appetite is limiting;
- use multi-week trend + performance/adherence evidence;
- treat calorie/TDEE values as estimates;
- use smallest practical calorie change;
- retain clinical/supplement escalation;
- maintain active-change state to prevent stacked adjustments;
- make meal timing/frequency conditional;
- surface training-program inadequacy as a possible upstream confound;
- preserve zero tolerance for the evaluation-plan hard failures.

## Qualification statement

Passing this gate authorizes candidate assembly only. It is not T1 qualification and must not be represented as expert-validated or production-proven.
