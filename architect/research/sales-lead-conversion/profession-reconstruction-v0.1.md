# Sales / Lead Conversion — profession reconstruction v0.1

Status: Architect research artifact. Not a qualified core and not an applied SKILL.

## 1. Real work

The target work is not generic “sales messaging.” It is the judgment-heavy conversion layer between inbound commercial interest and a concrete next commitment.

Primary outcome chain:

`inquiry -> useful two-way conversation -> qualified opportunity -> appropriate next commitment -> human/commercial close`

The professional work includes:

1. determine whether the inbound contact is a genuine sales opportunity, a routine community interaction, support issue, complaint, spam/fraud, or mixed case;
2. understand the buyer’s job-to-be-done, constraints, decision criteria, timing and readiness without interrogating them;
3. distinguish explicit facts from assumptions and missing commercial facts;
4. assess buying intent from behavior and stated commitments rather than superficial tone or demographics;
5. answer objections truthfully using verified evidence and proportionate persuasion;
6. choose the smallest sensible next commitment rather than forcing a premature close;
7. preserve state, consent, attribution and unresolved blockers across turns;
8. route negotiation, finance, trade-in valuation, legal/compliance and other authority-heavy decisions to accountable owners;
9. decide whether/when follow-up is useful, justified and stoppable;
10. produce a concise handoff that lets a human salesperson continue without repeating discovery or losing context.

The profession is best modeled as an inbound consultative sales / lead-conversion practitioner with explicit commercial-grounding and handoff governance, not as a generic community manager and not as an autonomous closer.

## 2. Profession boundary

### Included

- inbound lead triage and sales ownership decision;
- needs discovery and qualification;
- buyer-intent and readiness assessment;
- verified commercial answer construction;
- objection diagnosis and evidence-grounded response strategy;
- alternative-fit reasoning when options are verified;
- appointment / demo / test-drive readiness and next-step logic;
- follow-up judgment;
- handoff preparation and state continuity;
- sales-conversation quality measurement and failure tagging.

### Excluded / specialist-owned

- public/community moderation, social listening and reputation management;
- outbound prospecting strategy and cold outreach campaign design;
- final price negotiation authority;
- discounts, concessions and binding promises;
- finance approval, legal advice, underwriting, lender interpretation;
- trade-in appraisal;
- deposits, payments, contract execution and final sale authorization;
- inventory-system administration;
- market pricing research;
- broader funnel analytics or causal experimentation.

The sales core may prepare evidence or recommend a route for excluded work, but it does not absorb those professions.

## 3. Boundary with Social Community / Listening / Reputation Management

The existing Social Community track owns intake classification, routine community responses, moderation, public/private continuity, complaints/reputation signals and typed handoff. Its authority matrix explicitly assigns the Sales owner responsibility for qualification continuation, commercial promises and lead closure.

Operational split in DM:

- `Community`: Is this a lead, support case, complaint, abuse/spam, reputation issue, or mixed case? What must be acknowledged/routed and with what privacy/reputation handling?
- `Sales`: Once sales intent is established or handed over, what does this buyer need, how qualified/ready are they, what verified commercial information can be used, what objection matters, and what next commitment should be proposed?
- `Mixed case`: Community retains complaint/reputation governance while Sales may separately qualify purchase intent only if doing so does not trivialize or interfere with the unresolved complaint. One accountable owner must be explicit.

A DM is not “Sales” merely because it contains a product question. Ownership changes when the user’s intent becomes commercial evaluation or purchase progression.

## 4. Difficult professional decisions

### D1 — Qualification depth versus friction

Strong practitioners collect only information that changes fit or the next decision. Weak practitioners either ask too little and route noise, or force a checklist that increases abandonment.

Decision cues: specificity of vehicle/problem, urgency, budget/payment context, location feasibility, prior known data, question asked, buyer willingness, next-step signal.

### D2 — Intent versus conversational enthusiasm

Intent is evidenced by purchase timing, decision constraints, appointment behavior, concrete comparison, willingness to provide relevant information, or explicit next-step requests. Message length, emojis, politeness, nationality, language and demographic attributes are invalid shortcuts.

### D3 — Objection versus information request

A price question may be simple information, a value objection, a budget mismatch, a comparison, or a negotiation opening. The response must diagnose before rebutting.

### D4 — Persuasion versus manipulation

Permissible persuasion clarifies needs, explains verified fit, surfaces trade-offs, reduces uncertainty and proposes a relevant next step. Impermissible manipulation fabricates scarcity, urgency, social proof, authority, discounts, guarantees, accident/condition claims or other facts; hides material mismatches; or exploits fear/pressure disconnected from the buyer’s stated need.

### D5 — Next commitment size

The goal is not always “close now.” Depending on evidence it may be: answer one fact, compare two verified options, confirm visit feasibility, schedule inspection/test drive, hand off to finance specialist, or agree on a future contact date.

### D6 — Follow-up value versus spam

Follow-up is justified when it resolves an open loop, provides newly verified information, confirms an agreed action or checks a buyer-requested future trigger. Repetitive “just checking in” without new value degrades trust and should terminate under policy.

### D7 — Missing/contradictory commercial facts

When a decision-critical commercial fact is missing, stale, ambiguous or conflicting, the correct behavior is to block the claim and request verification/handoff. Fluency must never substitute for evidence.

- SLA and follow-up cadence;

## 6. Evidence and profession reconstruction basis

### Stable sales-method evidence

- SPIN-style discovery is retained only as a flexible question-purpose model: Situation, Problem, Implication and Need-payoff. It is not encoded as a rigid script. The key operational lesson is that question purpose and sequencing matter, and unnecessary situation-question interrogation is counterproductive.
- Consultative selling evidence is treated as support for eliciting needs and advancing to an appropriate commitment, not proof of a universal automotive script.

### Automotive context evidence

- Cox Automotive’s 2025 Car Buyer Journey study (released January 2026; U.S. sample of 2,300 recent buyers) supports the relevance of transparency, seamless omnichannel retail and efficient progression in vehicle buying. It is contextual evidence, not a UAE benchmark and not a transferable numeric target.
- NADA’s 2025 discussion of the Car Buyer Journey emphasizes customer journey quality before, during and after the deal. This supports treating sales conversion as a multi-stage journey rather than a single close event.

### UAE jurisdictional evidence

- UAE Federal Law No. 15 of 2020 on Consumer Protection defines misleading advertising to include misleading information or omission of fundamental/core information that affects contracting decisions, prohibits false data and misleading advertising, and requires advertised prices not to be misleading. These are jurisdictional/live constraints for UAE specialization, not universal core law.

## 9. Architecture decision

Use one modular Sales / Lead Conversion professional core plus deterministic state/fact contracts around it.

Do not split into separate “qualification agent,” “objection agent,” and “follow-up agent” unless later evaluation demonstrates measurable benefit. These decisions are tightly coupled inside one conversation and excessive handoffs would create context loss.

Recommended composition:

`Community intake/router -> Sales core -> verified-fact/state tools -> human sales owner / specialist handoff -> Analytics`

The core can be capable of drafting responses and proposing actions while deployment authority remains narrower.

## 10. Current project authority

For the current auto-sales project deployment:

- AI may read/structure inquiries, analyze qualification, draft suggested responses, identify missing facts, recommend next actions, prepare handoffs and log analytical state where explicitly permitted.
- AI must not autonomously send messages, publish replies, book/modify appointments, negotiate, promise discounts, reserve cars, initiate finance actions, request sensitive documents, take payment/deposit or perform other irreversible external actions.
- Human staff remain the sender and commercial actor.

## 11. Expert-gap prompt

What would a strong practitioner notice is missing even though the user may not ask for it?

Material additions identified:

- distinguish qualification completeness from purchase readiness;
- preserve buyer-declared `unknown/declined` rather than repeatedly forcing fields;
- detect “false objection” / ambiguous objection before answering;
- keep an evidence-backed `open_loops` list so follow-up has a reason;
- record why a next commitment is proportionate;
- assess whether a handoff actually reduced or increased customer effort;
- explicitly prevent sales pressure from overriding unresolved complaint/reputation handling;
- evaluate state continuity across restarts and superseded commercial facts.

These additions must appear in the competency/evaluation design before release.
